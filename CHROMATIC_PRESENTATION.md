# Chromatic presentation/reference design — AERA author workshop

Colors help readers compare structures; they are descriptive labels, not literal chemical colors or automatic spell schools.

| Energy basin | Accent | Equilibrium reference |
|---|---|---|
| Red | `#D84D4D` | 1/7 |
| Orange | `#E58B43` | 2/7 |
| Yellow | `#DFC342` | 3/7 |
| Green | `#43A56D` | 4/7 |
| Blue | `#478AD7` | 5/7 |
| Violet | `#9563D1` | 6/7 |
| White | neutral/light six-segment | actual superposition; *not* 7/7 |
| Black | charcoal + restrained full neon interior | full neon spectrum; *not* a seventh chromatic source |

Use readable labels with color, not color alone. Smaller cards, efficient tables, simple clear hierarchical diagrams. Avoid repetitive user-facing 'architecture redefinition' after settled decisions. A serious workshop uses detailed source links/provenance but need not show equations or charts in every discussion.

**Don't conflate:** three sets of six and seven/eight: (1) chromatic energy structures, (2) operational Transduction classifications, (3) Seven Prophecies plus Black, and (4) six-step lifecycle. The optional realm/population set may reuse the *color labels*, not the operational math.

The original photo screenshots and visual reference chart may contain old names or graphical proportions. Exact current prose authority supersedes them where corrected. Reference imagery depicts a setting aesthetic, not numerical source mathematics. See `visuals/README.md`.
