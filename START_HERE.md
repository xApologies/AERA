# Start Here — How an author uses AERA

**AERA gives you a consistent magical structure, not a prewritten novel.** You need not be a mathematician. You may use the original mathematical references directly, or have a knowledgeable collaborator/AI retrieve definitions and show how your ideas fit them. You retain creative authority.

## Minimum constraints to inherit

- AERA's Genesis foundational model: six chromatic energy structures R/O/Y/G/B/V and White superposition.
- Transduction as the common magic mechanism; the universal **Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release** lifecycle.
- The foundational mathematical and operational terminology and its domain distinction. A setting cannot replace Genesis with unrelated mana mechanics and still call that *the unchanged AERA foundation*.
- The World Clock's **five-layer** organization. The author chooses what gets populated in each layer.
- The Eight Prophecies and unique Black Prophecy as established history **when writing inside AERA's shared fictional continuity**. Do not repeat the singularity. A personally independent story can use the framework's tools; its relationship to shared continuity must be stated by the author.

## Author choices — not gaps to solve

- How the six chromatic energy structures are manifested: abstract energy basins, navigable native realms or other compatible patterns. The author chooses native species, or none.
- Whether local biology can consciously contact Genesis Field, how many basins are accessible, or whether Shaping is purely engineered.
- Optional Shaping interface: Binding Circles, tattoos/fractal manifolds, stones, cards, shared-existence bonds or a custom compatible design.
- Starscape neon positions, equilibrium conditions, planets, gravity, geography, ecology, technologies, institutions, historical period.
- Individual characters, arcs, conflicts, local chronologies, plot and narrative resolution.

## Example: author wants a card-wielding transductionist

The author chooses physical cards as the local Shaping interface. Their cards might encode geometrical scaffolds or organize a manifold, but this is **a proposal to be specified**, not an existing universal rule. The author still applies all six Transduction stages; chooses source energy basins; defines constraints and fatigue. This is **not automatically Raeon** (which is an established cultural game, separate from the hypothetical card-casting interface).

## Example: author wants no environmental monsters

Choose a constrained equilibrium Starscape. In this realization, local runaway Genesis collapse is absent or too limited to make endemic ELDRIS, storms or `domai`. Persistent chromatic-prism dungeons may still be available as environmental structures independent of monster genesis.

## Example: author wants a mathematically celebrated civilization

Use MK147's Institutions of Knowledge, sigil engineering, Alchemy, maegi, Raeon, Prism, `vaelum`, and material/economy models. All are **available**, not mandatory. Different worlds can use tribal societies, future technologies, or other forms. The artist may never need to read the underlying mathematical manuscripts in order to write a great scene.
