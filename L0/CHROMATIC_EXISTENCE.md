## Superposition and equilibrium

White is the resultant/stable **superposition of the actual six chromatic configurations**. One Starscape may have non-equivalent neon positions from another, and both may have stable White composition. **Equilibrium is NOT equivalent to six identical contributions or fixed x/7 positions.** Do not universalize 1/6 equal segments from an illustrative diagram. A chromatic realm might be portrayed as approximately **80% dominant basin + 20% scaffold**, e.g. six-color strips for MK231's invented realms; that is **one optional realm-realization illustration**, not universal Starscape math.

The author uses a Möbius-like conceptual picture: one presentation is White chromatic superposition; following the topology to its other orientation describes Black as the complete neon spectrum. Kira represents the manifestation of Black in the MK231 story. **Black is not a seventh colored basin or a fixed 7/7 energy tier.** Precise Möbius composition mathematics is source-dependent and remains OPEN where marked by `_bricked`.

## Neon disequilibrium and environmental Genesis effects

The author's world design permits an overall persistent Starscape with *localized* Genesis instability when a chromatic singularity is displaced into neon flux. Illustrative MK231 example: Yellow reference 3/7 ≈ 0.428571 versus a proposed elevated Yellow state of ~0.7193. Suitable instability can yield runaway recursive Genesis collapse, **ELDRIS**, **Genesis storms**, temporary **domai**. **Numbers are illustrative; no universal instability threshold has been authored in this thread.** AERA authors may set a Starscape in constrained equilibrium and not use endemic collapse-driven monsters.

**Dungeons are a separate environmental prism concept:** persistent Genesis structures that stratify/sort basin expressions, and can be included whether or not a world has collapse-driven monsters. Do not equate every dungeon to an ELDRIS source.

## Basin realization is author-configurable

- **Energy-only**: MK147 predominantly treats R–V as structural energy basins available to Transduction.
- **Realm realization**: MK231 chooses independent/non-ordinary continuous realms, one chromatic identity dominant in each, with native race populations and White shared material realm.
- **Other realizations**: permitted if consistent with underlying energy organization; don't presume every author must use Kira's six races.

This is the exact AERA distinction: **basins are mandatory; inhabited realms are optional**. Access to each basin is a separate biological/technical author choice.
