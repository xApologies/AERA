# Rainbow Road — Genesis-relational transport

**Foundational name retained:** Rainbow Road, named by the author. **Existing mathematical source** in `_bricked`; no new transport mathematics is invented in this archive.

### Three source-defined terms

| Term | Existing role |
|---|---|
| **Corridor** | Mathematically admissible route/continuation |
| **Portal** | Typed realized transfer/traversal over an admitted Corridor |
| **Rainbow Road** | Persistent ordered composition/history of Portals and domain-spanning transport; path history participates in identity |

The source formalizes path/compositional and history-bearing **information transport** independently of downstream physical/cultural semantics. Admissibility, route preservation, geometric relationships and transport limits are sourced to `_bricked`, not invented here.

## Author-selectable physical travel

AERA fantasy may realize chromatic realms as traversable environments or bridges connecting two different worlds within a White Starscape. A human may enter Red on material World A, traverse the non-ordinary realm, and emerge at an exit connected to World B. Destinations and paths may be mapped by native inhabitants but not known to travelers. Local geographic travel can occur in the chromatic realm; its independent temporal correspondence is a **setting-specific** author choice, not a universal mathematical conversion law.

There can be *many* exits, potentially to many material worlds. Rainbow Road gives structure for admissible connectivity/route history; the author decides specific gateways and transport interfaces. In AERA **Rainbow Road does not mandate gateways, interplanetary travel, separate realm clocks or a single portal spell**. No one must magically sense planets across light-years to use a known mapped route.
