# Genesis access — fantasy-engine permission and author choices

The **Layer One fantasy premises** that make AERA into *fantasy* are (1) conscious organisms can interact with the Genesis Field and its chromatic energy structures, and (2) compatible material inscriptions and engineered structures can interact with Genesis directly. This is not a claim about biological neurons or ordinary real-world materials.

The author discussed a biological model of **Genesis channels developing in the brain** that connect conscious attention to a person's locally occupied Genesis/energy identity. This is an author's possible evolution/awakening explanation. **There is intentionally NO universal law fixing how any species couples to the six basins.** AERAs with conscious casters and AERAs using primarily engineered sigils can both be valid settings.

### Author-selectable access cases

- Evolved Genesis-channel capability in a species; conscious operator learns to use it.
- A personal awakening that activates or opens previously dormant access.
- Inherited biological/genetic coupling to one, several, all, or none of R/O/Y/G/B/V basins.
- A Binding or another suitable geometry granting/establishing access.
- No conscious Genesis access even though the civilization uses sigils, devices, infrastructure or Alchemy.

An organism has its own local Genesis organization/identity in the **fictional framework**, but this does not mean every human is automatically a master of all six basins or every elf intrinsically casts fireballs. Authors define the actual capabilities and costs.

## Respect source scope

If an AI needs to formalize coupling geometry, use the original source definitions and ask the author what has actually been chosen; otherwise preserve OPEN. Intentional freedom of implementation is not an unfinished AERA foundation.
