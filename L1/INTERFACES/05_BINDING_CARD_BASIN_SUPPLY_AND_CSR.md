## Shared requirements, different capacity constraints

A Shaping's chromatic requirements are shared by its Binding and operational Card realizations. **Continuous Spatial Resolution (CSR; DG-0031) requires Orange, Yellow, Blue and Violet.** This supersedes the older source catalogue's Orange/Yellow assignment. The current [master catalogue](../SHAPINGS/README.md) already contains the four-basin selection.

A CSR Binding deploys its prepared Shape/Hold geometry through PSSP, with the practitioner resolving energy into that geometry. The existing roughly 60% personal Binding-allocation example remains illustrative. Practitioners normally have access interfaces for all four required basins; whether access Bindings consume additional bandwidth and whether that cost is included in the 60% remain OPEN.

Native White-superposed energy can supply a projected Binding manifold as an emergency or backup. This heavily draws on finite personal reserves and does not indefinitely replace direct chromatic access. White remains the superposition of six basins, not a seventh ordinary basin. The source's auxiliary-fuel analogy is not a new mana meter, quantitative conversion or permission to bypass an active Card's locks.

Skilled practitioners may supply multiple compatible Bindings or native Shapings from the same basin, subject to access, control, throughput, installed bandwidth, admissibility and cumulative fatigue. The source's CSR-plus-Fireball Binding example shares Blue and is conditional on those requirements.

An active operational CSR Card exclusively occupies O/Y/B/V across all that practitioner's interfaces. Any Card, Binding, native Shaping or `aen` access overlapping those basins is blocked. Inactive Cards occupy none; ownership has no framework inventory cap. Cards do not consume permanent Binding allocation. Card topology generally reduces the operator's control burden relative to a Binding, but does not remove fatigue. Precedence against an already-running conflicting Shaping remains OPEN; no forced cancellation protocol is added.

## Flight endurance and deployment

Ordinary-speed sustained CSR flight has illustrative upper-duration bands: low endurance/skill approximately 1–2 hours, moderate 3–5 hours, high 5–8 hours. These are source working examples, not universal guarantees or formulas. Lower speed generally reduces fatigue accumulation, while higher speed increases it; load, maneuvering and other Shapings also matter. Direct basin access does not remove the need for rest and recovery. Rapid long-distance flight may leave an `aithren` unable to defend against eldris.

Optional expedition practices and relay logistics are in [CSR and dimensional expedition equipment](../../L2/TECHNOLOGY/CSR_AND_DIMENSIONAL_EXPEDITION_EQUIPMENT.md). Exact flight, relay and bandwidth equations remain OPEN. MK163 character loadouts and price tables are retained only in the source archive, not prescribed as AERA rules.
