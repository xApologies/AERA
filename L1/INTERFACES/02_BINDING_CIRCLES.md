# Binding Circles — MK147 mechanics inherited by AERA

## Recovered source
MK147 `live-model/04_SYSTEMS/BINDING_VAEN.md`: a Binding Circle is a specialized physical sigil and identity-resolution scaffold. `vaen` are unusual identity-bearing resources, not ordinary elements, and are **not the only possible identity input**. Inputs may bear multiple identities. A circle selects admissible identity/identities and resolves them into a person or object. Binding acquisition is **separate from active Shaping**.

The installed reusable architecture subsequently deploys through **phase-state shadow projection (PSSP)**, like a compact spiky sphere expanding into a constrained manifold and maintaining **Shape and Hold**. Concept, energy organization, Manifest and Release remain practitioner-dependent. The same Binding can produce different efficiency and control according to conceptual depth. Fatigue is physiological/endurance-like, not a mana bar.

## Newly adopted AERA interface rule
Installed Bindings persistently occupy part of the individual's **finite local Genesis bandwidth**, constraining the total installed repertoire. A Continuous Spatial Resolution (CSR) flight Binding occupying roughly **60%** is a *working illustrative case*, not a universal formula or cost. Bindings allow specialization and reduce the need for direct field-construction expertise, but still require conscious Genesis coupling.

`live-model/04_SYSTEMS/SIGIL_ENGINEERING.md`: `ru’ne` are discrete physically inscribed orthogonal marks; complete sigils require substrates. Arrays combine sigils for coordinated effects. Do not conflate a physical Binding Circle, the stored Binding, and its active PSSP Shaping.
