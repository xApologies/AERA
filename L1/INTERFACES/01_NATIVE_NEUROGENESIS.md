# Native Genesis and Neurogenesis

**AERA Layer One fantasy premise:** humans are born with microscopic Genesis crystallization in the brain. A branching network consists of continuous filaments, each woven from six distinguishable strands, Red through Violet, like six threads twisted into one yarn. `Neurogenesis` is AERA's name for this fictional biological Genesis-channel development; it is not real-world neuronal neurogenesis. Penrose/Hameroff quantum consciousness ideas are speculative inspirations, not established evidence for Genesis coupling.

Even an untrained individual may learn elementary internal Genesis-energy movement. Full direct Transduction is difficult: the practitioner must conceive the result, consciously construct and hold the required admissible manifold, organize energy, manifest and release. A sufficiently knowledgeable and capable practitioner can perform **any admissible Shaping they can form and sustain**, not a finite installed list. Multiple simultaneous fields may use the same basin; they still consume energy, attention, effective bandwidth, Resolution, and induce fatigue. Architects are an engineering specialization, not the only possible native practitioners.

## Chromatic potions — working biology
A chromatic potion stimulates growth of its corresponding strand in the existing six-strand network. Growth forms irregular, **porous radial crystalline shells** connected by branches pushing outward from the core, not detached colored dots or sealed perfect rings. As the network thickens, chromatic surface exposure and neural space competition change. The [current AERA default](../NEUROGENESIS/01_CHROMATIC_CHANNELS_AND_POTIONS.md) is **one dose per basin in Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet order**, with recovery, neural adaptation and conscious acclimatization between administrations. This supersedes the earlier three-dose default and unresolved order; other regimens remain author-selectable. Even one massively overdosed potion can cause severe neural injury or death; no fixed quantity or dose-count safety threshold is established. Potions are expensive. Higher-density basins may be useful for stabilizing topology, not simply a linear magical power ranking.

The distinct [White Awakening Potion and `aen`](../NEUROGENESIS/02_WHITE_AWAKENING_AND_AEN.md) now define integrated neural/body Genesis biology and six trained, variable cutaneous access channels. These are distinct from the innate six-strand filaments and earlier fixed skin/tattoo interface examples. Global card basin exclusivity also blocks corresponding `aen`; native capacity limits and the six-stage lifecycle remain.
