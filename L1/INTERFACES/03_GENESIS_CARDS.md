# Genesis Cards — MK727 mechanics inherited by AERA

## Recovered MK727 source
`live-model/01_MAGIC.md`: a physical **maege-glass** Genesis Card embeds a field manifold, consciously activated/coupled through personal Genesis energy. The card handles prepared topology, while the practitioner directs the Shaping and bears fatigue. Idle maintenance is a small trickle; burst effects draw discretely; sustained fields/summons draw continuously. Multibasin cards fail if a required basin exhausts. Cards may be carried, placed or suspended near the practitioner; stationary separation can weaken coupling; cards can be damaged. Exact range and failure mechanics are OPEN.

**Ordinary card constraint:** at most one active conduit per basin per practitioner (R/O/Y/G/B/V). Two active cards cannot share any required basin. Six concurrent ordinary cards is possible only for six pairwise disjoint single-basin cards. Inactive cards can be carried without occupying basins.

## Newly promoted AERA-wide rule
The MK727 ordinary-card rule was originally *book-specific*. The author now establishes that an activated card's basin anchor is **exclusive across ALL interfaces** for the practitioner. Example: a Red+Orange card prevents a Red+Green card and prevents native Red-dependent manifold construction while the anchor remains. Native Genesis otherwise allows simultaneous same-basin Shapings. Whether an already-running native Shaping prevents activation of a conflicting card or must be released first is OPEN. Do not invent forced cancellation.

## Manufacturing and profession
Industrial sigil arrays can flash blank maege-glass cards with encoded manifolds. **Cardsmiths engineer cards; Architects/Runesmiths design and inscribe the sigils and workbenches.** An orthogonal workbench exposes an isolated, *coupled* PSSP view of the card's manifold, not an independent copy or automatic editor. Cardsmiths use mathematical expertise, professional tools, Genesis crystals/dust and identity-bearing `vaen` to inspect, modify or engineer manifolds; failures can fracture cards. Basin-set compression is possible at trade-offs, exact formulas/recipes OPEN.

**Scope exclusions:** MK727's 1,245-card catalogue, prices, individual spells, Kaira's Black Cards, and novel-specific economies are not universal AERA mechanics. MK727 Black Cards use separate exceptional concurrency; do not apply ordinary six-basin restrictions automatically.
