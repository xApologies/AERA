# AERA — Three Principal Transduction Interfaces

**Layer One; AERA author decisions, 2026-10-13.** Three principal, optional methods for carrying out the same six-stage lifecycle: Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release. These are not six chromatic energy basins or six operational Transduction domains, and authors can develop additional compatible interfaces.

| Method | Prepared architecture | Fundamental constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Native Genesis | Practitioner consciously constructs and holds arbitrary admissible manifolds | Very high conceptual and control burden, energy, fatigue, Resolution, Bandwidth |
| Binding Circle | Identity resolution installs reusable topology; PSSP deploys Shape/Hold | Persistent occupancy of finite personal Binding bandwidth |
| Genesis Card | Maege-glass encoded manifold, activated by conscious Genesis coupling | Exclusive occupation of all required chromatic basins across *every* interface |

A skilled native transductionist can sustain several independent Shapings drawing from the same basin if resources suffice, unless that basin is occupied by an active Genesis Card. Card restrictions are **global to the practitioner**, not just card-versus-card. A Binding's bandwidth footprint and a card's basin anchor are distinct limitations.
