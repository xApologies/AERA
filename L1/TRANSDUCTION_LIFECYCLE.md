# AERA — Universal six-stage Transduction lifecycle

**ESTABLISHED, AUTHOR-LOCKED.** The universal operation remains the same regardless of Shaping interface, species, institution of education or civilization. This lifecycle predates the evolved Genesis Field in Genesis Point material and was **retained** through the expanded Genesis mathematics. Use the *newer* field/energy interpretation below, not automatically the early pre-Genesis text.

1. **Conceive** — Establish the practitioner's conceptual model of the intended result: what it means and how it operates. A remembered picture of fire and a chemically informed model of combustion provide different concept precision.
2. **Shape** — Construct the constrained local Genesis-field geometry, manifold, boundaries/topology and admissible scaffold.
3. **Hold** — Maintain the Shaped field's structural coherence and persistent topology. Reusable Bound geometries may **automate both Shape and Hold**.
4. **Resolve** — Introduce/organize the appropriate basin energy into the held field scaffold and commit its admissible energy configuration. Energy need not derive from a single basin if topology requires more than one.
5. **Manifest** — Consciously collapse/realize the structured configuration under the intended concept into a perceptible/interacting Shaping.
6. **Release** — Finish the conscious collapse/realization and disengage/release the active constraints appropriately. Manifest and Release are especially affected by conceptual precision and efficiency.

`Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release`

There are **exactly six operations**. Do not insert an extra activation step into the universal lifecycle because a local interface (e.g. card or stone) requires interaction; activation may realize existing Shape/Hold architecture without changing the six stages.
