# Catalogue authority and unresolved records

The [single master catalogue](SHAPINGS_MASTER.csv) is installed byte-for-byte from the author's 2026-11-25 reconciliation package. Its 1,245 records retain source IDs, all 65 columns, empty fields and status. Read the [catalogue introduction](README.md) and [integration audit](../../provenance/upstream-reconciliation-001/INTEGRATION_REPORT.md).

## How to read the records

`required_basins` contains the supplied catalogue configuration. Colors in price/rank columns describe source progression and economics, not additional required basins, operational domains or a universal power ladder. The catalogue contains 1,016 INDUSTRIAL_BINDING and 229 SUMMON records; those original record types do not erase the distinction between a realized Shaping and its native, Binding or operational Card interface. Existing [interface rules](../INTERFACES/00_READ_FIRST.md) govern any use.

Author selection of this catalogue does not promote every candidate effect or source price into universal law. The preserved `status` values are 990 ALPHA candidate — domain-normalized v0.2, 232 ALPHA candidate, 15 LOCKED, six WORKING and two APPROVED CONCEPT — costs/rank expressions/basins OPEN. Even LOCKED is a source status with its original scope. MK157/illi story choices remain historical data. They do not become AERA story canon.
