# Six operational Transduction domains (NOT the energy basins)

AERA accepts the following six **operation categories** as established. They describe *what kind of interaction is being performed*, not where a basin's energy originates and not which race/color may use them.

1. **Persistent Topology / Coherence–Decoherence:** stabilization/repair/persistence versus destabilization/degradation of organized topology. Healing is here, but not all of this domain is healing. Ordinary Decoherence is generally less practical/efficient than Coherence; MK231's amplified Black pairing is exceptional.
2. **Dimensional Geometry:** operations involving geometrical/dimensional relationships and their appropriate constraints.
3. **Genesis Field Topology:** direct organizational/topological interactions within the Genesis Field.
4. **Genesis Expression:** organized manifested expressions of Genesis capability.
5. **Resonance:** coupling, oscillatory organization, reinforcement and sustained interactions.
6. **Chirality:** organizational grammar, orientation/admissibility and related structural interaction.

Source terminology: [MK157 Transduction](https://github.com/xApologies/MK157/blob/main/canon/TRANSDUCTION.md), and [MK231 core stack](https://github.com/xApologies/MK231/blob/main/canon/00_THE_STACK_AND_BOUNDARIES.md). **Do not invent an exhaustive taxonomy of operations from these short names**; the source-level math and each author's application decide valid implementation details.

## Absolute separation

- **Six chromatic basins**: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet = energy structures, L0.
- **Six operational domains**: the six descriptions above = operation classifications, L1.
- **Seven Prophecies**: magical foundation/constraint descriptions, not this list.
- **Six lifecycle stages**: temporal operational sequence, not these domains.

There is **no general Red=Persistent Topology, Orange=Dimensional, etc. mapping**. A Dragon might specialize individually in one Transduction domain without that changing its native Red energy identity. Basins are not magical schools or person-level character ranks by default.
