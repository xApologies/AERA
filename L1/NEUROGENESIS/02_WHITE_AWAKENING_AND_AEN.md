# White Awakening and `aen`

**Awakening Potion** is the selected name for the distinct White Genesis potion. A transformed person is **Awakened**. White is the coherent superposition of six chromatic basins, not an ordinary seventh basin. Taking ordinary chromatic potions or learning native Transduction does not automatically make someone Awakened. Whether prior chromatic dosing is required for White Awakening remains OPEN.

## Biological reconstruction
The White potion remakes the individual's existing biology and Genesis architecture at the energy/structural level, rather than merely enlarging neural channels. Brain tissue and Genesis crystalline structures become **functionally fused/integrated** in a unified neural architecture, rather than separate crystalline filaments passing through ordinary tissue. The body gains Genesis-integrated physiology. Natural skin pigmentation, eye color and recognizable individual identity are preserved. Skin gains an unusual healthy multilayered optical depth; hair becomes fuller/healthier; eyes show faint internally trapped luminescence in their original color, most apparent in darkness. Typical fictional phenotype is leaner, denser athletic musculature, modest height increase, more angular/symmetric features and subtly larger orbital proportions. Fat stores may be substantially consumed during restructuring. The author's 8–11% body-fat example is **illustrative fiction**, not a universal healthy real-human percentage or mandatory outcome.

Kaira's exceptional MK727 white skin, Black/Neon spectral eyes, Affliction and unique restoration are **not** generic Awakened traits.

## Four-layer skin Genesis interface
Inspired by the functional multilayering of cephalopod skin, but **not** by literal pigment-based camouflage. Four provisional functional layers: (1) biological surface, (2) chromatic Genesis anchors, (3) chromatic organization/separation, (4) body/neural integration. The exact tissue histology remains OPEN. This gives the Awakened a **body-wide controllable energy-access surface**, not merely a colored visual effect.

## `aen` (exact spelling, lowercase)
`aen` are six distinct **cutaneous Genesis energy-access channels**, one each R/O/Y/G/B/V. They can manifest anywhere suitable on the body; wrist training is a tradition, not an anatomical restriction. The [current training progression](03_AEN_TRAINING_AND_FATIGUE.md) begins with six ~1 cm dots on either wrist, practiced R→O→Y→G→B→V. Each manifestation ordinarily restarts from a dot, then extends into an open arc encircling the wrist, connects into a closed ring, and only then widens into a band. Direct expansion from dot to broad patch is impractical. Closure reduces resistance relative to an equally extended open arc but still requires focus and fatigue; wider bands increase potential throughput and maintenance cost. Advanced branching and extensive body-surface control remain trained capabilities, with full sleeves exceptional. The skin is not a fixed tattoo; geometry is consciously variable and must accommodate movement.

Greater controlled surface area can support **greater energy throughput for native Shapings and prepared Bindings**, potentially turning seconds-long channeling into effectively immediate energy loading for a valid manifold, but this is **not a linear unlimited-power law**. **Operational Genesis Cards receive no `aen` throughput boost:** energy flows through the card, whose occupied basins cannot simultaneously be accessed by that practitioner's `aen`. All these uses contribute to the same cumulative basin-specific fatigue. Cognition, geometry, fatigue, admissibility, field stability and energy organization still constrain Transduction. Ordinary Genesis-card basin exclusivity remains global: an activated card occupying Red blocks that practitioner's Red `aen` as well as direct Red manipulation. Precedence with an already ongoing Shaping is OPEN.

## Fictional longevity

The [selected longevity ranges](04_LONGEVITY.md) give White Awakened a **typical 400–500-year lifespan**, with exceptional historical individuals reaching approximately 700 years. Do not collapse the typical range to a 450-year average or treat 700 as ordinary. Integrated biology can extend life even without learning Shaping; internal Genesis maintenance is distinct from combat mastery. These are approximate design ranges, not fixed death ages or guaranteed benefits.
