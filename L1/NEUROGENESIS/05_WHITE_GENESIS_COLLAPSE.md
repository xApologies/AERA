# White Genesis Collapse
Status: author-established fictional mechanism, 2026-11-25. Available to trained White Awakened; not automatic upon Awakening. Read with 02_WHITE_AWAKENING_AND_AEN.md and 03_AEN_TRAINING_AND_FATIGUE.md.

## Activation and morphology
All six independent cutaneous aen must be simultaneously active as closed rings. Widening is not required. Coherently normalize their outputs to the Violet reference and enter White superposition. The recursive collapse configuration is singular; recursion depth does not vary with mastery. The physical envelope assumes a humanoid black recursive Genesis containment silhouette, with intermittent White internal luminescence and subtle shadow-like drag in movement. Clothing and armor contours disappear when incorporated. No separate chromatic armor ranks, external shield, or new operational domain. Individual chromatic access remains possible.

## Normalization and sustenance
Native output rises Red → Violet at comparable extraction effort. Violet establishes the reference; progressively greater extraction effort is needed Blue → Red to equalize outputs. Equal output is not equal extraction. Illustrative scale 1–20, Violet reference 4, all six normalized to 4; no physical units or exact coefficients selected. Coherent participation and equal output are required, not merely six active rings. Symbolic bookkeeping (provisional notation, not L0 derivation): Q_R = Q_O = Q_Y = Q_G = Q_B = Q_V = q; extraction effort P_R > P_O > P_Y > P_G > P_B > P_V at the reference configuration. Increased normalized supply reinforces existing topology, not deeper recursion. Exact output/effort relationship remains OPEN.

## Damage and pseudo-armor
Physical and energetic impacts are sustained by the collapsed topology. Localized weakening can accumulate and eventually breach the physical structure. The practitioner can increase extraction through all six rings, maintaining normalization, to reinforce compromised topology and sustain themselves through damage. This accelerates fatigue. No invulnerability, fixed durability statistic, resistance coefficient or damage conversion is established. Reinforcement of collapse is distinct from healing actual biological injury; exact breach and injury transfer mechanics OPEN.

## Physical envelope and selective incorporation
The Genesis envelope extends slightly beyond the body; clothing, armor, sheathed weapons and sufficiently encompassed carried objects normally participate. Incorporated armor does not independently increase collapse robustness or directly receive incoming damage; the collapsed topology sustains damage instead. Incorporated equipment rematerializes when collapse resolves. Skilled practitioners can deliberately exclude selected objects, e.g. a sword and sheath, retaining access to them while collapsed. Beginners generally incorporate such objects. Exact envelope extent, boundary conditions, effects of a breach on material continuity, and behavior of incorporated enchantments remain OPEN. Do not infer unlimited incorporation or storage.

## Fatigue and endurance
Existing narrow-ring maintenance examples: Red ~31 hours, Violet ~23 hours. Selected average baseline collapse endurance: ~17 hours for a trained capable Awakened under ordinary sustainment, without significant extra Shaping or damage reinforcement. This is a design benchmark, not a derived value or universal maximum. Combat reinforcement may reduce endurance to ~11–13 hours as an illustrative example only; actual duration depends on damage, reinforcement, prior fatigue, concurrent Shapings and individual endurance. Lower basins require greater extraction effort but have lower inherent chromatic difficulty; fatigue may be approximately balanced, not necessarily equal. Violet generally exhausts first; other activity can change the limiting basin. Exhaustion of any required basin ends collapse; unexhausted basins remain accessible. Fatigue is cumulative and basin-specific across ring maintenance, extraction, reinforcement, native Shapings and Bindings. No mana pool. Optional bookkeeping: F_b = F_prior + F_collapse + F_reinforcement + F_shaping; no exact additive physiological law or fatigue coefficients established.

## Concurrency and mastery
Native Shapings and compatible installed Bindings can operate during collapse, subject to control, throughput and fatigue. Active operational Genesis Cards are incompatible because they exclusively occupy required basins across interfaces; keep Cards inactive during collapse. No forced activation/cancellation precedence is specified. White Awakening is expensive in MK163; affluent practitioners often favor Bindings. Frontline combatants, including Augmenters, are likely users; no mandatory profession or wealth requirement beyond setting-specific access. Mastery improves endurance, control, selective incorporation and reinforcement, not recursion depth. Augmentation remains distinct.
