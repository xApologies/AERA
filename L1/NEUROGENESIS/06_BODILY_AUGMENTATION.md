# Bodily augmentation through Transduction
Author-approved upstream migration 2026-11-25. Source: MK163 WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/PROFESSIONS/AUGMENTER.md. Locally anchor a transductive event to the practitioner's body. Compatible operations include Resonance and Coherence; organized energy temporarily alters biological state, increasing muscle mass, strength and structural support. The effect persists for an interval as organized energy is used and dissipates; continuous active channeling throughout every movement is not required. The enhancement fades to native state. Duration and physiological fatigue are distinct. No fixed duration, growth coefficient, replenishment law or invulnerability. Professional applications include melee, martial training and equipment use; compatible utility/healing Shapings do not redefine the profession.
