# Innate Genesis filaments and chromatic potion grafting

People in the selected AERA human model are born with microscopic crystalline Genesis filaments distributed through the brain like a branching secondary vascular network. **Each filament is one continuous woven yarn of six chromatic strands** (R/O/Y/G/B/V), not six independent networks or large crystalline blocks. They permit conscious Genesis coupling and basic internal energy movement with practice. Full direct Shaping requires knowledge and construction of an admissible manifold.

A basin-specific **Genesis potion** selectively stimulates growth of its corresponding strand: a Red potion grafts Red, etc. The crystalline strand expands radially, forming **irregular porous approximately concentric growth shells**, with branches passing through prior layers and occasional runaway protrusions into outer layers. Other strands inhibit and constrain growth. Later grafts fill voids and extend outward, increasing overall filament diameter and exposed chromatic access surface. Avoid perfectly sealed circular rings or random isolated color dots.

**AERA default regimen:** one dose of each chromatic potion, in Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet order, with recovery, neural adaptation and conscious acclimatization between administrations. This is a simple author-inheritable option, not a universal dose count. Exact timing, market price, formula and quantity remain author-defined. High-density basin surface may be valued for stabilizing certain field topologies, not as an absolute magic-power hierarchy.

**Alchemical formulation is safety-critical in the fictional setting.** Genesis-active mass/concentration, growth rate, cumulative crystalline volume, biological accommodation and recovery matter. Even a single massively overdosed potion can cause runaway growth that disrupts normal brain function, causing severe injury or death. An alchemist controls the material load and stabilization; the carrier volume alone is not a reliable dose measure. There is no fixed universal gram amount, medically safe dose, or published biological equation. Earlier three-dose/fourth-injury/fifth-death statements are superseded as AERA defaults; an author may choose such thresholds for a specific world.

Neural growth can increase available access, but cannot replace the six-stage lifecycle, manifold knowledge, Resolution, Bandwidth, admissibility, fatigue or conscious control.
