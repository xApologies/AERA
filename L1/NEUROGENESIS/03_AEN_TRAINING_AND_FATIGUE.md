# AERA — aen formation, closure, expansion, endurance
Status: author-established working canon, 2026-10-13. Supplements existing White Awakening document.

Six cutaneous `aen`, one per Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Violet, can manifest anywhere suitable on the body. Wrist training is tradition, not anatomy. Form six ~1 cm dots on either wrist. Practice R→O→Y→G→B→V. Every manifestation ordinarily starts again from a dot, even after mastering a ring; exceptional experts may complete the sequence almost instantly.

**Required geometry:** dot → open arc encircling wrist → endpoints connect into closed ring → widen the closed ring into a band. Expanding a dot directly into a broad patch is impractical. Open arcs resist extension elastically, increasingly as the gap closes. Closure reduces maintenance resistance compared with an equally extended open arc, but still requires focus and fatigue. After closure, widening the ring increases surface area and elastic contraction toward its narrow width. A full sleeve is possible for exceptional practitioners; three inches is an illustrative width, not a hard limit. Wider bands increase potential energy throughput and maintenance cost.

Chromatic difficulty increases R<O<Y<G<B<V for equivalent geometry, without implying automatic magical power rankings. Author-approved illustrative chart values are 10,14,21,29,43,50, **not** physical coefficients or a proven exponential law.

Fatigue is one cumulative existing basin-specific physiological system: native Transduction, Bindings, Genesis Cards, aen maintenance, expansion and throughput all contribute. Exhaustion of a basin prevents further compatible use until recovery. Illustrative practitioner might maintain a narrow Red ring for ~31 hours and a narrow Violet ring for ~23 hours, but a wide Violet band much less; not universal limits. Training builds closure, chromatic control, width and endurance.

**Important correction:** aen boost energy loading for native Shapings and prepared Bindings, but NOT operational Genesis Cards. A card exclusively anchors its basin(s) and draws energy **through the card**. An Awakened's aen cannot simultaneously access card-occupied basins or boost card throughput. All still generate fatigue. Ongoing-Shaping/card precedence remains OPEN.

No new lifecycle, mana, exact fatigue/area equation or mandatory ranks.
