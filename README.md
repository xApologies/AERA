# AERA — The High Fantasy Framework

**First GitHub publication corpus | 2026-10-10 | cumulative conversation snapshot v0.3.0**

AERA is a **modular high-fantasy framework** grounded *within its fictional canon* in Genesis mathematics. It gives an author the five-layer **World Clock**, the six chromatic energy structures and White superposition, **Transduction** as universal magical operation, and reusable optional technologies, ecosystems, disciplines and narrative examples. AERA is not a mandatory cast of characters or a prescribed novel.

**This repo is the first curated, self-contained AERA chat-workshop corpus.** It is **NOT** a wholesale copy of the mathematical, novel, game or worldbuilding repositories. Full upstream-corpus consolidation is a later, separately authorized migration. The current corpus deliberately preserves historical source pointers, explicit placeholders, differences between immutable foundations and author choices, and the original v0.2 AERA thread package.

## Four concepts that must NEVER be conflated

| Structure | What it is | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Six **chromatic energy basins** | Discrete foundational energy structures R/O/Y/G/B/V, singularity neon states and White superposition | L0 existence and energy |
| Six **operational Transduction domains** | Categories of magical operations and Shapings, **not** colors | L1 operation classification |
| Seven foundational **Prophecies of Magic** + eighth **Black** | Concepts/constraints of magic plus unique historical singularity event | AERA magic and shared fictional history |
| **Six-stage Transduction lifecycle** | Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release | Universal realization of magic |

The **author controls** whether an energy basin manifests as a realm, what organisms live there, whether a person has conscious Genesis access, what Shaping interface they use, their technology and social organization, and the characters/plot. AERA requires its core foundations, not the example implementations.
