# MK231 native realm realization — example-specific species and capabilities

These are the six particular fantasy populations chosen for **this seven-book saga**, not the necessary occupants of AERA's mandatory R–V energy basins.

| Native realm | Population | Existing story-world magical identity |
|---|---|---|
| Red | Dragons | collectively versatile across six operational Transduction domains, **individual dragon specializes in one**, not all six |
| Orange | Phoenixes | fire/light/flight, exceptional Coherence, healing, energetic reproduction and native rebirth |
| Yellow | Primordials | matter interaction; inorganic, organic and process variants (stone/metal; plants/fungi; fluid/fire/storm etc.) |
| Green | Kitsune | local realization of admissible potential via Genesis Resolution/Bandwidth/Chirality; **one literal fox tail**, eight apparent trailing Genesis echoes |
| Blue | Leviathans | vast energy throughput, durability and spatial scale; native Blue geometry can be nonordinary |
| Violet | Unicorns | native unification/mastery of **all Seven Foundational Prophecies**; advanced mathematical/prophetic culture |
| White | Humanity | broad compatibility with Binding structures and inter-realm travel, not automatic innate magic |

## Selected Blue Leviathan forms (MK231 visual selection)

Six accepted: **Abyssal Crown, Tidevault, Skyweaver, Stormmantle, Cloudbreaker, Horizon Leviathan**. Previously contemplated Worldback #10 rejected. Horizon's selected native visual: **variation #8 Fractal Spine** (not earlier #9 Vanishing Continuum image); moving horizon/persistent/vanishing continuum conceptual identity retained. Latest 2×3 humanoid female Leviathan design is exploratory. No mandatory seventh Blue classification.

## Biological/cultural boundaries

Elf-like and dwarf-like humans are physiological/brain-developmental forms *within humanity*, not separate automatic magical races. Chromatic species may culturally learn human-like morphology for White diplomacy without surrendering their independent origins. No one-to-one 'race = Seven Prophecy' mapping exists. A Dragon individually selecting Chirality does not redefine the Red chromatic basin as Chirality; these remain independent axes.

## Healing hierarchy (MK231 example)

Ordinary source comparisons gave Phoenixes outstanding healing, Organic Primordials second, Coherence-specialist Dragons next, and skilled humans through suitable Bindings thereafter. The Black Phoenix's amplified status is an exceptional story event; this hierarchy is not a mandated ranking for all possible AERA organisms.
