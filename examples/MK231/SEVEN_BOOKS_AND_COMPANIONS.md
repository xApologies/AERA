# MK231 — Seven-book narrative colors and companions

## Absolute distinction: BOOK LABEL ≠ NATIVE REALM COLOR

**Newest explicit author choice for book labels:** Books I–VII ascend Red→Orange→Yellow→Green→Blue→Violet→White. Companion acquisition stays Phoenix→Kitsune→Dragon→Unicorn→Primordial→Leviathan. Do not relabel the creatures' native realms to match their book color.

| Book | Narrative book color | Black companion / role | Native chromatic realm | Relationship route |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | Red | Phoenix | **Orange** | unprecedented Black Phoenix egg, first exceptional Binding |
| II | Orange | Kitsune | **Green** | consent of a developed conscious Kitsune |
| III | Yellow | Dragon | **Red** | Black Dragon egg, possible guardians/war/diplomatic service |
| IV | Green | Unicorn | **Violet** | independent conscious Unicorn; trust + prophecy guidance |
| V | Blue | Primordial | **Yellow** | Genesis crystal-lattice cradle (egg analogue) |
| VI | Violet | Leviathan | **Blue** | Black Leviathan egg; community trust |
| VII | White | Completed sixfold union | White and all | culmination, meaning of healing existence remains under development |

This supersedes earlier **book labels** Orange→Green→Red→Violet→Yellow→Blue→White; that earlier mapping was native companion **realm** colors and became confused with narrative book titles. The original acquisition order and native chromatic identities remain intact. An earlier exploratory consideration of swapping Primordial and Leviathan was **not locked**; the currently accepted final acquisition order uses Primordial V, Leviathan VI.

Book I starts with a White-native Afflicted blacksmith and the Phoenix egg, not immediate entry into Orange realm. Orange may be visited later in the book. Book II visits Green; Book III Red; Book IV Violet; Book V Yellow; Book VI Blue; Book VII closure. The six Books I–VI need not be identical challenge templates; each Black existence/realm has an independent history and Kira must earn trust.

**Six independent Black companions** = three literal eggs (Phoenix, Dragon, Leviathan), one Genesis crystal cradle (Primordial), two conscious existing beings (Kitsune, Unicorn). Never flatten them into six spells or six property objects. Binding is shared existence with personal agency, not ownership.
