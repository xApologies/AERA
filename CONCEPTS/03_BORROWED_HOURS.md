# III. Borrowed Hours — duration and temporal obligations

**Operation:** Transfer duration. **Constraint:** Temporal obligations.

**ESTABLISHED.** 'Hours' is poetic, not a unit restriction: seconds, years, centuries, millennia or a sustained interval of existence can be relevant. The recovered MK231 foundation explicitly connects this concept to MK Ultra's **Time Shell**, localized temporal Resolution/Bandwidth and persistence. This conversation reiterates the Time Shell connection. The original concept does **not** grant unrestricted time travel or chronology rewriting.

**INTERPRETIVE.** A duration-sensitive operation raises questions about what persists, what interval is affected, what is transferred or allocated, and what temporal dependency or consistency condition follows. 'Obligation' means the temporal consequences cannot simply be ignored. It does **not** yet mean a lender, payment, debt collector, conservation equation or interest rate exists. Those would be additional author decisions.

**Historical exploratory example:** the old foundation considered accessing a prior configuration five seconds earlier to influence an outcome three seconds later, without ordinary physical backwards time travel. This was explicitly exploratory, **not** a proven operator or locked spell.

**Author-facing questions:** What duration matters? Whose state persists? Is duration genuinely transferred or only reorganized? What temporal consistency must hold? Is the Time Shell invoked? These are questions, not a compulsory time-accounting subsystem.

**OPEN:** source/sink of borrowed duration, conservation, repayment, retrocausality, exact Time Shell operators and realm-clock conversion.

**Boundary:** independently evolving clocks in MK231 are a story-specific realization. They are not the universal definition of Borrowed Hours. Duration is distinct from consent (Covenant Gardens), connection (Thousand Threads), and spatial path (Cartographers).
