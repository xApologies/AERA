# V. Covenant Gardens — magic and living existence

**Author-level working description:** Magic's relationship with living existence / Conditions and mystery of living participation.

**ESTABLISHED — scoped author correction v0.9.0.** This pillar concerns the relationship between magic and living systems, and the question of existence itself: what is life as a system within existence, and what is magic's role in that relationship? It is distinct from Living Art's focus on expression, organization and evolution of living systems.

**INTERPRETIVE.** Whether existence requires a witness or experience remains an open philosophical question. It is not a cosmological law, an observer-created universe, a biological consciousness requirement, or a universal consent operator. Living participation does not make mountains sentient or every interaction a contract.

**Historical provenance:** Negotiate agreements / Consent and obligation was the earlier category. Contractual magic is superseded as the defining pillar. Agency, consent and obligation may still matter in a specific story; Kira's independent Black companions remain story examples. A sigil and its substrate do not literally consent. Earlier negotiated-power wording and source qualifications are preserved in [the reconciliation record](../provenance/PROPHECIES_SEMANTIC_PASS_v0.9.0.md) and its historical text receipt.

**Author-facing questions:** What living system participates? What relationship with magic is being described? What distinguishes existence, experience and expression? These are interpretive prompts, not mandatory mechanics.

**OPEN:** the witness/experience question and any formal account of living participation. Earlier covenant enforceability, withdrawal and consent mathematics remain historical source-open matters; this correction does not solve or require them.

**Naming:** Framework Covenant Gardens remains plural; the accepted literary draft is The Covenant Garden, singular. Both are preserved; naming variation awaits an author decision.

**Boundary:** Covenant Gardens concerns magic's relationship with living existence; Living Art concerns living-system expression; Thousand Threads concerns existing relations; Borrowed Hours concerns temporal continuity. No new mathematical axiom follows.
