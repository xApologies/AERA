# V. Covenant Gardens — agreements, consent and obligation

**Operation:** Negotiate agreements. **Constraint:** Consent and obligation.

**ESTABLISHED.** The original concept concerns agreements and negotiated relationships. Later author discussion broadened its interpretation to how geometries and biological systems can coexist, while preserving genuine **agency and consent** when conscious beings are involved. The MK231 source explicitly warns against inventing spirits, contracts with mountains, or mandatory legal agreements among all objects.

**INTERPRETIVE.** For conscious participants, negotiation is not identical to coercion or ownership: a relationship may require each party's participation, permissions and ongoing commitments. Kira's Black Kitsune and Unicorn shared-existence relationships are useful **story examples** of independent agency and trust; they do not establish that every friendship is a spell or every Binding is a legal contract.

For non-conscious structures, 'agreement' is at most an analogy for **compatibility, mutual constraints, or coexistence** until a formal operation is specified. A sigil and its substrate do not literally consent. The concept can still help an author think about obligations among interacting structures, but personhood must not be silently projected onto geometry.

**Author-facing questions:** Who are the parties? Are they conscious? What permissions are granted? What persists after an agreement? Which obligations are meaningful? What happens when participants withdraw or conditions change? These are interpretive prompts, not new universal laws.

**OPEN:** formal covenant mechanism, enforceability, withdrawal rules, how non-conscious constraint coexistence relates to the original negotiation operation, and any exact Genesis mathematics of consent.

**Boundary:** Covenant Gardens concerns agreement/permission; Thousand Threads concerns existing relations; Borrowed Hours concerns duration. They can overlap in a story without being the same concept.
