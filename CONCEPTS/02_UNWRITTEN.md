# II. The Unwritten — valid statements, grammar and meaning

**Operation:** Express valid statements. **Constraint:** Grammar and meaning.

**ESTABLISHED.** Magical expression has meaningful organization. It may involve physical `ru’ne`, formalized sigils, language, an utterance, an architecture of Genesis geometry, or a practitioner's concept. The original source explicitly warns that speech, inscription, semantic grammar and geometry are **not automatically the same mechanism**. This is not a requirement that all magic be written or spoken, and not an unrestricted true-speech system.

**INTERPRETIVE.** *Grammar* can be read as the organization of a particular expression: components, ordering, relationships and well-formedness within the chosen interface. *Meaning* concerns what that organized expression specifies or intends to realize. *Valid* means the expression must be admissible under the selected Genesis and Transduction constraints, not merely linguistically elegant or visually convincing. These are explanations of the labels, **not** a newly authored universal Genesis parser.

Examples: a physically inscribed sigil can express a local Genesis operation; a Binding Circle may provide a prepared geometric scaffold; a person with better conceptual understanding can realize a comparable Shaping more efficiently; a civilization might teach incantations or diagrams as an interface. None proves a universal translation between spoken words and topology.

**Author-facing questions (not new rules):** What carries the expression? What organizes it? What does it mean? What makes it admissible? How does Transduction realize it? The six-step lifecycle remains Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release; these questions do not add stages.

**OPEN:** formal Genesis language, syntax-to-field mapping, whether words themselves can couple to Genesis, universal primitives, and exact semantic validation mathematics.

**Boundary:** The Unwritten concerns **meaningful expression**; it does not itself define the energy basin, a profession, or a unique Shaping interface.
