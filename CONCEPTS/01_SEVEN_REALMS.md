# I. The Seven Realms — existence and compatibility

**Operation:** Exist across realm structures. **Constraint:** Compatibility.

**ESTABLISHED.** Six chromatic energy structures (Red through Violet) participate in White superposition. This is a structural account of *existence*, not a map of seven compulsory inhabited worlds. The six neon reference landmarks are 1/7 through 6/7, but actual configurations may vary. Energy basins are not Transduction operation categories, spell elements, or character ranks. White is the combined superposition, not an ordinary seventh chromatic basin.

**Compatibility** asks whether structures and interactions can coexist across the relevant configurations. MK147 uses chromatic structures primarily as energy basins; MK231 additionally realizes them as independently inhabited realms with White material existence. Both are valid AERA expressions. Authors may select other compatible realizations. The 80% dominant/20% scaffold realm chart is an optional MK231 illustration, not a universal formula for Starscapes.

**INTERPRETIVE.** An author can ask which structures must coexist for a proposed organism, device, realm passage or Shaping to function, and which incompatibility would prevent it. This is a useful design test, not a new mathematical compatibility operator.

**OPEN:** exact universal compatibility algebra, particular species permissions, gateways, local clocks, and map topology. Rainbow Road remains upstream L0 transport mathematics; the author's travel mechanism is an L1 implementation.

**Boundary:** existence/compatibility (Seven Realms) differs from relationship modification (Thousand Threads) and spatial adjacency (Cartographers).
