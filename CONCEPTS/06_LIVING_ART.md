# VI. Living Art — cultivation, biology and ecology

**Operation:** Cultivate magical life. **Constraint:** Biology and ecology.

**ESTABLISHED.** Magic can be **grown, bred, grafted or cultivated**. The recovered foundation explicitly allows some organized magical expressions to be **alive without being conscious**. It also explicitly says not all magic is alive and not every organism is a spell. No new mana, spirit substance or universal sentience is introduced.

AERA already distinguishes non-Genesis, Genesis-adapted and Genesis-dependent organisms. Genesis-adapted life benefits from a specialized interaction but can survive without it; Genesis-dependent life requires suitable interaction for continued function. A luminous fungus with Red/Orange channels could be an alchemical resource, and may qualify as `vaen` only if it bears a suitable unusual Genesis-relevant identity. Brightness or concentration alone is not enough.

**INTERPRETIVE.** 'Cultivate' invites an author to consider development, reproduction, metabolism, ecological niches, symbiosis and the environmental conditions that support a living magical structure. The Genesis Field is the inherited substrate; the author chooses actual biology and constraints. Living Art may interact with material-science Alchemy, Architect `velis` growth fields and Genesis ecosystems without replacing those systems.

Examples from source discussions: Phoenix energetic reproduction/rebirth, Organic Primordials, Genesis-adapted plants, living Shaping constructs and cultivated forests. These examples are not assigned exclusively to Living Art and do not imply identical reproductive biology.

**Author-facing questions:** Is the structure alive? Is it conscious? What maintains it? Does it reproduce or grow? What ecosystem supports it? How does it respond when Genesis conditions change? What makes it biology rather than a temporary visual effect?

**OPEN:** general reproduction equations, classification of all living Shapings, genetic/chirality coupling, ecology dynamics and universal criteria for conscious life.

**Boundary:** ELDRIS are separately defined recursive-collapse entities, not simply Genesis-mutated animals. Living Art does not require every organism to be a spell.
