# AERA: Seven Concepts of Magic — semantic expansion v0.4.0; reconciled v0.9.0

These concepts describe what magic is within AERA, independent of their later literary use as prophecies. They are NOT the six chromatic energy basins, six operational Transduction domains, six casting steps, seven spell schools, or seven compulsory species. The eighth Black Prophecy is a separate event whose literary framing is reconciled separately from story history.

Evidence notation: **ESTABLISHED** = explicitly author-confirmed or preserved in MK231 canon; **INTERPRETIVE** = explanatory author-facing reading, not a new mathematical law; **OPEN** = not yet settled by source. Examples do not create new framework requirements.

| Concept | Operation | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| The Seven Realms | Exist across realm structures | Compatibility |
| The Unwritten | Express valid statements | Grammar and meaning |
| Borrowed Hours | Transfer duration | Temporal obligations |
| Thousand Threads | Modify relationships | Existing connections |
| Covenant Gardens | Magic's relationship with living existence | Conditions and mystery of living participation |
| Living Art | Expression and evolution of living systems | Structural organization, continuity and transformation |
| Cartographers | Alter spatial adjacency | Geometric consistency |

Historical names: `Six Realms` was corrected to `Seven Realms`; `Borrowed Hours` is not `Bard Hours`; `Covenant Gardens` is the currently recorded MK231 plural, although an older screenshot and recent spoken recitation used singular. Do not silently override naming authority.
