# VII. Cartographers — spatial adjacency and geometric consistency

**Operation:** Alter spatial adjacency. **Constraint:** Geometric consistency.

**ESTABLISHED.** This is the fantasy-facing geometry concept: how locations, boundaries, neighbors, connected regions and nonordinary continuous geometries can be arranged or altered while respecting the Genesis mathematical foundation. It relates to manifolds, topology and spatial relationships, not just drawing geographical maps.

**INTERPRETIVE.** Adjacency means which locations count as neighbors or can be connected by an admissible spatial structure. Changing adjacency is not necessarily the same as moving an object through ordinary distance. Geometric consistency means the chosen construction must satisfy its own topology, boundaries and allowed transformations; it cannot become an unlimited 'teleport anywhere' permission simply because the story calls it magic.

Author examples: realm environments with nonordinary geometry; connected passages through a chromatic realm; a spatially locked engineered structure; a Genesis projection with defined boundaries; a navigator discovering an existing exit route. These are **examples of geometric questions**, not proof that each is a Cartographers Shaping. The Horizon Leviathan's moving continuum was explicitly a **possible** example, not an established operator.

**Rainbow Road** is already an upstream mathematical transportation framework. Cartographers is a broad concept of spatial adjacency. Do **not** claim they are identical or reconstruct Rainbow Road equations in this semantic pass. Authors decide whether realms are traversable and how gates, corridors or portals are physically realized.

**Author-facing questions:** What are the regions? Which are adjacent before and after? What topology is being changed? What boundary conditions and compatibility constraints matter? Is this a spatial change or merely a new relationship/information link?

**OPEN:** formal adjacency operators, portal geometry, route admissibility, dimensional metrics, travel-time behavior and exact L0 crosswalk.

**Boundary:** Cartographers is spatial adjacency; Thousand Threads is broader relational connectedness; The Seven Realms is structural existence and compatibility.
