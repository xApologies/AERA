# Cross-concept semantics and author tests

The seven concepts are **not** a compulsory sequence for a Shaping. They are descriptions of aspects of magic. One operation may implicate more than one; the seven do not map one-to-one to six chromatic basins, six Transduction operation domains, six lifecycle steps or seven races.

| Concept | Primary question | Not equivalent to |
|---|---|---|
| Seven Realms | What structures of existence can coexist? | A literal seven-planet map; an energy-to-spell taxonomy |
| Unwritten | What makes a magical expression meaningful and admissible? | Mandatory spoken incantations or universal true-name control |
| Borrowed Hours | What duration is transferred and what temporal constraints persist? | Unlimited time travel or automatic temporal debt |
| Thousand Threads | Which existing relation is being modified? | Creating arbitrary connections from nothing |
| Covenant Gardens | How does magic relate to living existence? | Mandatory contracts, observer-created existence or conscious geometry |
| Living Art | How are living systems expressed, organized and transformed? | Every recursion is biological life or consciousness |
| Cartographers | What spatial adjacency changes consistently? | Unlimited teleportation or generic relation modification |

## Three different example analyses — interpretive, not canonized spell recipes

### A. Sigil-activated infrastructure
- Unwritten: inscription geometry carries a meaningful structured operation.
- Cartographers: geometry and adjacency may matter if the infrastructure changes traversable space.
- Thousand Threads: existing coordinated sigils may form a relation, but do not automatically grant relation modification.
- No need to say the sigil 'invokes' all seven prophecies. The underlying energy basins and Transduction remain distinct.

### B. A Genesis-adapted luminous fungal patch
- Living Art: biological growth and ecological adaptation are examples of living-system expression.
- Unwritten: only if there is a meaningful expression mechanism; glow alone does not prove one.
- Covenant Gardens: the relationship between magic and living existence may be considered; fungi need not have human consent.
- Alchemy/vaen: material-science and identity-bearing resource systems are separate available technologies.

### C. Kira's shared-existence relationship with a conscious Black companion
- Covenant Gardens: magic and living participation are the current focus; consent and reciprocal obligations remain story-specific themes, not the defining universal pillar.
- Thousand Threads: existing shared identity/relational structures may be relevant.
- Living Art: expression and evolving organization can involve independent living beings; this does not imply universal sentience.
- Black Prophecy: a unique fictional historical event; NOT a universal eighth concept or compulsory AERA character template.

## The transduction separation firewall

- **L0 six chromatic basins**: where/what energy structures exist; R/O/Y/G/B/V, White superposition.
- **L1 six operational domains**: what sort of Transduction interaction is performed.
- **Six-stage lifecycle**: how a Shaping is carried out in time.
- **Seven concepts**: broad foundational dimensions of magic, its operation and constraint.
- **Black Prophecy**: independent foretelling of a mysterious singular manifestation of magic; its later story realization belongs to shared AERA fictional continuity.

No general one-to-one mapping between any of these sets is established. Do not invent one to make a tidy diagram.
