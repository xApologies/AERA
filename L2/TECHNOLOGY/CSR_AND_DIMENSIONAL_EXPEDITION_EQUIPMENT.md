## Dimensional equipment

The existing [Dimensional Ring master](DIMENSIONAL_RINGS.md) and [source reconciliation](DIMENSIONAL_RING_RECONCILIATION.md) govern conservation through inheritance reassignment and conscious recognition. Their mechanisms are unchanged. Equipment examples include ropes, lanterns, shovels and bridge sections; retrieval skill and organization matter under the Jenkins principle.

A bracelet is an available alternate form. The MK163 expedition example selects a leader's bracelet with greater practical storage capability than the group's ordinary ring; this is an optional equipment choice, not a universal bracelet-to-ring multiplier. Exact engineering, dimensions and comparative capacity remain OPEN. A backup ring carried by another member is optional; bearer, quality and contents remain author-defined.

Crossings may use ropes, anchors, tensioned crossings, mobility Shapings and equipment carried by dimensional devices. The source's no-hauled-boat choice belongs to its vaelum expedition, not a prohibition on boats in other AERA civilizations. Exact routes and crossing engineering remain OPEN. No pocket dimension, unlimited inventory, automatic sorting or storage-volume formula is introduced.

## Occupational and relay examples

Dungeon-delving `aithren` may choose CSR Cards to preserve installed Binding allocation for combat, `helkir` work or protection, while accepting four-basin exclusivity during use. Storm-chaser `vaelum` may instead employ dedicated CSR Binding flight specialists whose principal job is mobility and logistics. Source affordability and occupational preferences are setting examples, not mandatory equipment or professions.

In the relay example, a specialist flies outward and prepares a spatial-corridor endpoint. Another team member at the origin connects or opens the corridor, personnel transit, and the process can repeat. CSR flight is not by itself a complete corridor-construction specification. Required corridor Shapings, topology, connection mechanics, endpoint protocol, crew numbers, distances, deployment limits and fatigue logistics remain OPEN. The archived 300-mile example is hypothetical, not a selected range.
