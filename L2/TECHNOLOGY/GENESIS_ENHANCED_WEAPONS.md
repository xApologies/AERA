# Genesis-enhanced oversized weapons and impulse geometry
Author-approved migration 2026-11-25; available optional engineering model. Source: MK163 WORLD_CLOCK/L2_WORLD_CIVILIZATION/TECHNOLOGY/GENESIS_WEAPONS_AND_IMPULSE.md and PROVENANCE/WORKSHOP/LIVE_MODEL_006/SESSION_006/GENESIS_WEAPON_IMPULSE_GEOMETRY.md (2026-10-19).
Oversized swords (six to seven feet), mauls and axes may undergo sigil imprinting and enchantment. Accepted conceptual architecture: reduce experienced handling inertia and gravitational load independently; distinct impact recoupling with optional amplification. Personal augmentation may complement equipment. Reaction/recoil management and conservation are necessary. Anchoring to Genesis Field or Shield to displace apparent mass is a working hypothesis, not a solved topology. No fixed reduction, recipe, latency, energy cost or damage multiplier.
Geometric impulse J = p_final − p_initial = Δp. For a sufficiently isolated combined system Δp_weapon + Δp_target + Δp_GenesisField + Δp_wielder/surroundings = 0. Closed vector polygon is preferred; omitted interactions must be negligible or included elsewhere. Target impulse is its own momentum change. Preserve classical cross-reference J = integral F_net dt; F_avg = J/Δt; ordinary constant-mass p = mv and kinetic energy = mv²/2. Field-mediated changes must balance energy and momentum. Source example: 300 lb (~136 kg) maul, illustrative handling inertia 13.6 kg, 10 m/s; reduced-state 136 kg·m/s and 680 J versus full-mass equivalents 1360 kg·m/s and 6800 J. Differences 1224 kg·m/s and 6120 J require accounting. Not a universal specification or physical proof. Preserve unresolved coupling and recoil.
