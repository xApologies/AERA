# Genesis Diamonds — energy storage canon
**Source authority:** `provenance/technology-v0.6.0/sources/GENESIS_DIAMOND_MASTER.pdf` (seven pages). This is a source-preserving MK147 technology specification offered as an optional AERA civilization system. Reference charge times and dimensions are world-engine examples, not universal Genesis-field constants.

## Identity and containment
Genesis Diamond = **battery** / universal energy-storage infrastructure. The carbon diamond lattice is a stable containment geometry. The crystal remains diamond; stored Genesis energy remains energy. Neither transforms into the other. Energy occupies available configuration space inside the crystal. The diamond is a container, not the energy itself.

Any of the six chromatic basin energies may be stored. The crystal continually organizes occupancy into a stable distribution, compared by the author to a **quicksort-like** process. It does **not** retain a single uniform basin value throughout its volume. The conserved descriptive quantities are **total stored energy** and **average energy density**, organized in a stable occupancy distribution. This is the master’s qualitative storage model; do not fabricate sorting algorithms, charge equations or lattice Hamiltonians.

## Visible charge states
The colors below identify **average density/charge state of a diamond**, not the source basin of every energy unit inside it and not the six operational Transduction domains.

| State | Reference average density / appearance |
|---|---|
| Zero | Empty, clear diamond, no frost/color |
| Infrared | Below Red (< 1/7); frosted, clouded, diffuse; first visible charging state |
| Red | Around 1/7; clouded red |
| Orange | Higher density; greater clarity, orange |
| Yellow | Gem-quality clarity begins; topaz-like |
| Green | Stable high-density; emerald-like |
| Blue | Very high density; near-clear |
| Violet | Around 6/7; extremely clear, glass-amethyst |
| Ultraviolet | Above Violet (> 6/7); nearly invisible, identifiable by refraction/distortion/lensing |

**Infrared and ultraviolet are charge-state descriptions, not new mandatory energy basins.** Empty and fully charged are not to be confused with White superposition or Black neon identity. Source does not define a universal numerical upper ultraviolet bound.

## Stability and superposition
The master relates naturally superposed White energy organization to the comparative instability of isolated basin densities. The crystal organizes a stable average density, making concentrated Genesis energy more manageable for later use. Do not infer that every diamond contains identical chromatic fractions or that a charge-state color specifies its energy provenance.

## Charging times — source reference example
Reference target: a **1/4-inch chip**, empty → Red charge state.

| Charger basin access | Approximate time |
|---|---:|
| Red | 8 hours |
| Orange | 7 hours |
| Yellow | 5 hours |
| Green | 3 hours |
| Blue | 2 hours |
| Violet | 1 hour |

These are author-level economic/infrastructure figures, not physical constants or universal per-unit energy transfer laws. The master illustrates one hour of Red charging on eight days versus one hour for a Violet-access individual to achieve one Red chip; real daily schedules are author-configurable.

An infrastructure charging array may charge a comparable 1/4-inch chip empty→Red in about **five days**, favoring volume, consistency and scale over personal speed. This does not imply all large arrays have identical output.

## Size classes
| Class | Source reference size | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Chip | ~1/4 inch | Common personal battery |
| Circle Crystal | ~1–4 inches | Binding circles, advanced equipment, specialized systems |
| Infrastructure Crystal | ~1 ft × 1 ft × 1 ft | Major storage block |
| Greater Infrastructure Crystal | Larger than 1-ft cube | Strategic/regional energy reserve |

Charging time grows dramatically with volume; the source provides no general exponent or exact capacity formula.

## Author-level use
The device permits stored Genesis energy as a traded and manufactured infrastructure resource. The charging economy can reward greater chromatic access without requiring a specific monetary system or civilization. AERA authors may adopt the technology, modify world-specific reference sizes and costs, or omit it; do not overwrite the underlying distinction between lattice container and stored energy.
