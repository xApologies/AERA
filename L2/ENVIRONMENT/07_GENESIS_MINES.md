# AERA — Genesis Mines: monochromatic crystallization and oscillation
**Status:** author-established optional Layer Two environmental mechanism, 2026-10-13. Supersedes incompatible Genesis Point mining rules; original five-page PDF preserved as historical source in provenance.

## Definition
A Genesis Mine is a localized environmental Genesis-field configuration that **isolates exactly one** of the six structural chromatic energy basins (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue or Violet), in a prism-like separation analogous to but distinct from the multi-stratum configuration of dungeons. The mine is a persistent local field pocket, not merely a conventional ore deposit. A single mine's ordinary crystal output is **monochromatic**. Do not assume White or Black mines as default, nor equate a mine with a dungeon, domai, or separate chromatic realm.

## Oscillation and growth
The mine's local field oscillates cyclically. Oscillation events can nucleate/grow corresponding **Genesis crystals**. Harvesting removes crystals; subsequent oscillations may replenish them. **Equilibrium hard rule:** the field has a finite stable crystalline capacity/preferred local configuration. At equilibrium, further oscillations **continue but cause no additional net crystal growth**. Harvesting disturbs/reduces occupancy and permits replenishment during later oscillations. This is renewable but NOT unbounded resource generation.

The old source describes bead nucleation, marble-scale expansion and honeycomb/lattice organization during release; these are historical illustrative morphology, not mandatory AERA crystal size, growth kinetics, extraction method or geometry. Whether a single event fully restores missing crystals remains OPEN.

**Distinguish:** (a) internal field oscillation frequency, (b) recurrence period of hazardous release/growth events, (c) duration of an event, and (d) permissible worker exposure time. Qualitatively Red oscillates more slowly and Violet more rapidly; do NOT infer fixed recurrence intervals or frequency formulas. Schedules, yield, cavity size, saturation, recovery windows and mining logistics are author-defined. Mine cycles may be predictable enough to plan evacuation and harvest.

## Fictional exposure hazards
Exposure risk rises qualitatively R→O→Y→G→B→V. These are fictional Genesis-field neurological interactions, NOT actual ionizing radiation or medical dose guidance.

| Basin | Author's qualitative oscillation exposure example |
|---|---|
| Red | Tingling; generally tolerable brief exposure |
| Orange | Mild temporary numbness |
| Yellow | Headache; repeated oscillation exposure concerning; evacuation customary |
| Green | Dangerous; even one oscillation may cause permanent neurological injury |
| Blue | Severe; one oscillation expected to cause lasting neurological damage |
| Violet | Extreme; one oscillation likely fatal |

Red/Orange/Yellow general exposure is comparatively manageable but not universally harmless; chronic exposure may accumulate injury. Green/Blue/Violet require increasingly cautious work/exposure and recovery protocols. Work shifts, protective methods, neurological rehabilitation and mining policies remain author-selectable. The fact that an equilibrium mine no longer grows crystals does **not** make its oscillations safe.

## Exclusions and source migration
**REJECTED as AERA default:** Genesis Stones (replace with **Genesis crystals**); stone absorption or any need to absorb a crystal for environmental protection; will-based stone release/extraction; fixed 17-day schedule; stone-specific red resonance syndrome; obligatory penal mines; universal ELN stone-backed currency; old mandatory stone-fracture/dust rules. These may remain documented as historical Genesis Point content, not current AERA mechanics. Existing AERA Genesis crystal/dust/Alchemy, `vaen`, diamonds and domai rules retain their own authority; do not replace them with obsolete source claims.
