## Source discipline

The complete [12-page eldris master](../../provenance/environment-v0.5.0/sources/ELDRIS_MASTER_LRS.pdf) is preserved byte-for-byte. Its [page-labelled extracted text](../../provenance/environment-v0.5.0/sources/ELDRIS_MASTER_EXTRACTED.txt) is a retrieval aid; the PDF remains the original. Sections 0–18 are mapped in the [section register](../../provenance/environment-v0.5.0/MASTER_SECTION_MAP.md). Selected MK147 source files are preserved as text snapshots, with original paths and commit IDs in the [source register](../../provenance/environment-v0.5.0/SOURCE_REGISTER.md).

The master supplies eldris ontology and behavior. The domai lifecycle, effective dungeon containment and Wilds definition also use their named MK147 source records. Do not attribute those supplemental passages to the PDF when it does not contain them.

## Source-specific meanings retained explicitly

The master uses “dungeon-born” in its original framing, but also defines eldris through local instability in its general definition. AERA already accepted formation beyond dungeons. Both source wording and active scope are retained in the [authority notes](../../provenance/environment-v0.5.0/SOURCE_BOUNDARIES.md); this pass does not silently restore dungeon-only origin.

The master also contains an identity-persistence comparison with spirits. It is preserved in a dedicated source-context note, not deleted and not converted into a mandatory new afterlife system. Illustrative group sizes, nine-foot bodies, hour-long fights and the “jumps” notation remain examples or qualitative comparisons.

## What this pass does not alter

No changes to the five-layer World Clock, seven concepts, eight prophecies, Transduction lifecycle, six operational domains, or the chromatic energy structures. No new realm clock ratios, map dimensions, encounter tables or math operators. No changes to either constitution. ER Foam remains the existing tactile-surface placeholder. No upstream repositories are cloned.
