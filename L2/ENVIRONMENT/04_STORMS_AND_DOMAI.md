# Genesis storms and domai

**Primary supplemental authority:** MK147 `live-model/02_WORLD/STORMS_DOMAI.md`, accepted Increment 002. The eldris master explains the entity model; this dedicated source supplies the domai lifecycle and perceptual model. The distinction is preserved for future audits.

## 1. Genesis storm

A Genesis storm is a localized, weather-like disturbance of the Genesis Field. It creates unstable conditions with elevated potential for collapse.

A storm does **not** automatically become a domai. Storm-driven collapse can produce local/runaway collapse and eldris without a bounded environmental landscape forming. Suitable bounded environmental collapse can instead resolve into a domai. These are related possibilities, not a guaranteed three-stage progression.

The author chooses the local incidence, scale and civil response. The established chromatic-equilibrium model permits settings without an endemic storm or monster crisis.

## 2. What a domai is

A `domai` is a temporary bounded Genesis-event landscape: an **environment**, not a conscious organism. It can contain entities and resources without itself being an eldris.

The inherited basin classes are Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue and Violet. There is **no White domai** in this model. Its White structural core does not turn the surrounding environment into a White class.

## 3. Lifespan and perception

The source says lifespan **generally increases with basin**. That qualitative trend is retained; it supplies no numeric lifetime, universal expiration timetable or fixed size.

The internal geometry is anomalous/non-isometric. Nearby depth remains usable. As distance increases, ordinary depth information degrades toward a blurred, approximately two-dimensional, projection- or surface-like horizon. Long-range depth and distance become unreliable.

That describes the source's perceptual effect, not a claim that the entire landscape literally becomes a two-dimensional sheet. No fixed visibility distance is supplied. Do not substitute a training-map radius from another project.

## 4. Structural core, not a mind

The core is a **non-conscious superposed White Genesis Crystal**. It does not think, govern the environment or command eldris. Its role is structural. Eldris inside a domai are inhabitants or manifestations; they are neither its consciousness nor equivalent to its core.

This distinction matters in scenes and in technical descriptions. Locating or breaking the core is not, by definition, defeating a mastermind.

## 5. Two routes to unraveling

The source supports both **natural expiration** and **core destruction** as causes of unraveling/dissipation. Core destruction is not the only way a domai ends.

Unraveling is **progressive**, not instantaneous disappearance. The changed conditions create a final high-risk harvest and evacuation period. The source does not specify a universal safe interval, automatic expulsion of occupants, fixed collapse speed or immunity of collected materials. Those details should not be invented as source facts.

```text
Suitable bounded environmental collapse
  → temporary domai with structural White crystal core
  → natural expiration OR core destruction
  → progressive unraveling / final risky harvest and evacuation
  → dissipation
```

This is a readable summary of the inherited lifecycle, not additional lifecycle stages for personal Transduction.

## 6. Contents and resource value

Possible contents include eldris, Genesis crystals, Genesis Dust, White Genesis Dust, `vaen` and other rare resources. The existence of a harvestable output does not promise a particular amount, market price or entitlement to safely obtain it.

The resource model connects to Alchemy and expedition work. No separate mana material or universal game-loot system is added.

## 7. Optional MK147 response example

In the source civilization, Red and Orange responses may involve local `vaelle` and specialist aithren; Yellow and above can also involve kingdom military. Recognized vaelum may support operations. This is an example of how that civilization allocates capability, not a requirement that another AERA setting have nobles, a Crown, military organizations or identical escalation thresholds.

## 8. Exclusions retained from the source

Do not import Genesis Point's older permanent Domai Heart / Class-0 Chimera model as the current domai definition. Do not change a temporary landscape into a conscious creature. Do not attach MK157-specific training rewards, recurring event schedules or encounter numbers.
