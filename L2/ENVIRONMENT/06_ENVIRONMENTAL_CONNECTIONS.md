# Environmental connections — life, resources and civilization

This document makes the existing dependencies visible. It does not create additional mandatory social institutions or expand ER Foam beyond its placeholder.

## 1. Different origins of dangerous creatures

An eldris is a bounded persistent recursive-collapse entity. A Genesis-adapted animal is an organism with specialized Genesis interaction. A Genesis-dependent organism needs the relevant interaction for normal survival. These categories do not collapse into a universal monster category merely because each can be dangerous to a character.

AERA already documents non-Genesis, Genesis-adapted and Genesis-dependent life in [Genesis Biology](../GENESIS_BIOLOGY.md). Source-defined eldris are not ordinary animals, and a forest full of luminous plants is not itself evidence of an eldris event.

## 2. Material and identity-bearing outputs

A dungeon or domai may yield crystals, dust, flora and other resources. Genesis-active organisms can also supply Alchemy. `vaen` is the framework's distinct category for unusual Genesis-relevant identities/properties, not simply any high-energy material or every chemical element.

The original mushroom illustration links a living resource to material science and potentially to a suitable identity-bearing input; it does not make all luminous fungi the same resource. [Alchemy and vaen](../ALCHEMY_VAEN.md) preserves the Type 1–5 scaffold and resource distinction. No new recipe or dust yield is authored here.

## 3. Two professional environments, shared cultural identity

The existing [Culture and Games](../CULTURE_AND_GAMES.md) chapter preserves the `aithren` participatory culture: competence, discovery, contribution, technical curiosity and social belonging. Dungeon delvers work around persistent locations and mapped routes with comparatively safe exterior staging. Storm chasers work with changing Genesis-event conditions, direct encounters and resource extraction.

Restoring effective containment strengthens the inherited explanation of the dungeon community; it does not require every AERA story to use that community. `vaelum` and Accord recognition are available institutional examples, not universal command structures.

## 4. Preparation still matters in mapped places

A mapped dungeon can have changing admissible configurations. A known domai class can still have unreliable distant depth. An eldris family can be recognized only after meaningful traces or behavior are observed. These are established reasons why information exchange, guides, observation, preparation and skilled support matter without inventing a game difficulty function.

## 5. Infrastructure and food

The existing Architect/velis example pairs a localized growth-supporting field with Alchemist-prepared nutrient media. Its seven-harvest illustration is for a suitable crop under suitable conditions, not a rule for every plant. Territorial stabilization, protected agriculture, difficult livestock management and plant-based diets are linked examples of a particular civilization responding to environmental pressure.

No mandatory diet, production rate, monarchy, taxation scheme or military roster follows merely from using AERA.

## 6. Existing boundaries remain intact

This pass changes no seven-concept explanation or Black Prophecy. It changes no Transduction stage, operational-domain taxonomy, current book order or character powers. ER Foam stays **a projection of the Genesis Field used as a tactile surface**, with detailed source recovery deferred. Other source migration and repository cloning remain outside this package's authorization.
