# Domain context — existence, environments and local conditions

**Scope:** the existing AERA environmental framework. This is a terminology and dependency reference, not a replacement Layer Zero theory.

## Several uses of “domain”

| Term | Meaning in this corpus | Do not confuse with |
|---|---|---|
| Chromatic basin / singularity | A Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue or Violet energy structure participating in White superposition. | A combat skill, profession or operational Transduction category. |
| Inhabited chromatic realm | An author's realization of a basin-associated environment, with chosen geometry, inhabitants and access. | A requirement to use MK231's races or local clocks. |
| Starscape | The larger realized existence whose configuration and worlds the author specifies. | One mandatory planet or one fixed calendar. |
| Dungeon | A persistent geographic Genesis environment acting as a chromatic prism or set of strata. | A temporary domai or a one-time cleared game level. |
| domai | A temporary bounded Genesis-event landscape arising under suitable collapse conditions. | A conscious monster, a realm, or a political territory. |
| Political / territorial domain | A civilization's own geographic or administrative division. | A new chromatic energy source. |
| Operational Transduction domain | One of the six classifications of magical operations. | One of the six structural energy basins. |

## Chromatic setting context

[Chromatic Existence](../../L0/CHROMATIC_EXISTENCE.md) retains the six reference positions, Red 1/7 through Violet 6/7, actual neon configurations and White superposition. These are reference landmarks, not six mandatory fixed realized values for every Starscape. The source distinguishes equilibrium from simply giving six components identical proportions.

A Starscape can remain persistent as a whole while local Genesis instability occurs. Suitable neon disequilibrium can permit recursive collapse, eldris and Genesis storms, with bounded environmental collapse capable of forming a domai. The world-specific high-Yellow example belongs to MK231; no universal requirement makes Yellow the source of every AERA threat.

A constrained-equilibrium setting need not include endemic collapse-generated monsters. This does not require the removal of dungeons, ordinary wildlife or Genesis-adapted ecosystems. An author may include dangerous living organisms that are not eldris at all.

## Relationships, not a compulsory chain

A Genesis storm is a disturbance, an eldris is a persistent bounded collapse entity, and a domai is an environment. A storm need not form a domai to produce eldris. A domai can contain eldris without its core being their mind or commander. Dungeons have their own persistent organization and changing admissible configurations. These are related mechanisms, not interchangeable names or guaranteed consecutive stages.

The exact gateway, inhabited realm, temporal profile, frequency of storms, population of a dungeon, and form of local civilization belong to the author. Recovering these environmental definitions does not require designing those choices for everyone.

## Existing transport scope

[Rainbow Road](../../L0/RAINBOW_ROAD.md) remains the inherited transportation structure. Nothing in this environmental pass redesigns it. Optional physical realm passages and destinations remain author-selected realizations. A dungeon or domai is not automatically a portal to another world.
