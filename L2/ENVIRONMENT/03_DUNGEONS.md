# Dungeons — persistent chromatic environments

**Sources:** the AERA dungeon-prism interpretation already published; MK147's dedicated dungeon/world definitions; the eldris master's section 12. These sources contribute different parts of the description and are identified below.

## 1. Persistent environment, not a cleared instance

A dungeon is a persistent geographic Genesis environment. AERA describes its environmental function as a prism or stratified structure separating Genesis-field expression across the Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue and Violet basins. The inherited order is R→O→Y→G→B→V. A dungeon is not merely a finite checklist of game levels that disappears after the inhabitants have been defeated.

Its geography, number of routes, particular stratum layouts and interaction with its surrounding world belong to the author. Nothing here installs a universal floor count, map size or encounter schedule.

## 2. Effective containment

**Restore the source rule:** in the inherited MK147 dungeon model, **eldris are effectively confined to compatible strata**. The previous AERA phrase “tend to stay” weakened environmental confinement into apparent behavioral preference and is superseded by the source wording in this pass.

Effective confinement is the stated basis for comparatively safe staging areas and dungeon-support settlements outside an entrance. It does not establish that all dangers everywhere are confined: other local collapses, storms, wildlife and human events may still matter to a setting. No new escape exception, invisible-wall mechanism or containment equation is invented.

Containment of dungeon inhabitants and the possibility of eldris forming outside dungeons are compatible source statements. One describes a dungeon's organization; the other describes where suitable instability may produce an entity.

**Source:** MK147 `ELDRIS_DUNGEONS_DOMAI.md`, Dungeons section; also the existing audit ENV-01.

## 3. Oscillating admissible states

The eldris master says dungeons are structured Genesis environments with **oscillating admissible states**. Eldris are one possible output of that oscillation. A dungeon may consequently present a different configuration while remaining the same persistent environmental feature. [Master §12, pp. 9–10.]

This restores a missing environmental behavior; it is not a replacement game-spawn system. The source supplies no universal period, respawn timer, probability distribution, depletion rate or obligation to repopulate after every expedition.

## 4. Full source output range

The master names monsters, flowers, crystals, cores, rare configurations, treasure-bloom events and saturation events. It also gives explicit alternatives: no monsters and only crystals; a rare flower bloom; or a treasure-collapse event.

An author's dungeon can therefore support resource collection, research and observation as well as combat. That is available source behavior, not a guaranteed reward schedule. “Core” in the dungeon output list is not automatically the non-conscious White structural core of a domai; the source does not equate them.

The original possible population examples are preserved here without becoming requirements: 300 Red eldris, 20 Orange, 7 Yellow, one Green juggernaut, or 15 Green juggernauts. They illustrate variation, including potentially severe variation, rather than a fixed table. [Master §12, p. 9.]

## 5. Admissibility, not fairness

The master distinguishes an admissible configuration from a fair encounter. A dungeon is not obliged to match a party's preparedness or make its current population equally manageable. This is a worldbuilding distinction, not a numeric difficulty curve. It coexists with mappable geography and professional preparation: knowledge of a route does not make every configuration identical.

This does not give the assistant permission to add arbitrary hostile events. Use the author's chosen situation and the source's permitted behavior. [Master §12 continuation, p. 10.]

## 6. Dungeons without endemic monsters

The current AERA framework permits a persistent dungeon-prism without an overarching Starscape monster crisis and without eldris in the setting. The master itself supplies no-monster resource configurations; the wider AERA decision allows an author to use the environmental structure without adopting the endemic collapse threat.

A dungeon is not identical to a domai. It does not inherit a domai's finite lifetime or required White crystal core by proximity. The names and mechanisms remain separate.

## 7. Civilization-facing consequences

MK147's established implementation supports staging, supply, lodging, exploration and professional dungeon communities. A dungeon reaching Green, Blue or Violet strata may become an important regional economic anchor. The detailed social institutions and named settlements remain examples, not conditions every AERA dungeon must satisfy.

The framework already offers [aithren and vaelum](../CULTURE_AND_GAMES.md), [resource Alchemy](../ALCHEMY_VAEN.md), [Genesis biology](../GENESIS_BIOLOGY.md) and [settlement stabilization](05_STABILIZATION_AND_WILDS.md). Their precise use belongs to the story.

## 8. Tournament boundary

MK147's King's Tournament is a source example of dangerous dungeon racing: reach the bottom, obtain a token and return to the surface; navigation, avoidance and logistics matter. The source's qualification structure and point scale remain in its preserved text snapshot. This environmental pass does not make that competition, monarchy or reward scale universal to AERA.
