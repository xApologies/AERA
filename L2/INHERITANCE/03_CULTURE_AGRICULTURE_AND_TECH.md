# MK147 source inheritance: cultural and world-engine examples

**Scope:** These are world-specific examples available to AERA authors; they are not mandatory civilization laws.

## Cultural continuity
- `aithren`: participation/community identity rather than a job title. Includes dungeon delvers and Genesis-storm chasers. Status follows contribution, curiosity, competence and discovery. Analogies to racing infields, 1980s computing communities and operational field teams are author references, **not imported technology**.
- Chainmail Robe: Kira's practical clothing solution becomes a profession-neutral Transductionist symbol and catalyzes **Day Zero of the Age of Knowledge**. Central realization: professions share Transduction, while conceptual understanding is an improvable efficiency bottleneck. This is **MK147 historical canon**, not a mandatory AERA age sequence.
- Source horizon: Age of Knowledge on roughly millennium scale, with earlier estimate ~1300 years before Age of Wonder conditions. Treat as source estimate, not universal timetable.

## Agriculture
- Source society predominantly vegetarian, with meat a specialty; **17 agricultural slices × 13 crops** as redundancy/staple architecture. Do not infer the complete 221-item catalogue from this summary; it must be source-recovered if needed.
- Source categories: greens, grains, roots, fungi proteins, legumes, aromatics, herbs, orchard fruit, berries, nightshades, dense calories, salads, plant fats/oils, preservation/fermentation, sweeteners, spices, emergency crops.
- Fantasy trade names are in-world labels; parenthetical real-world names are author translation keys. Genesis Point/Sanctuary framing is not inherited into MK147.

## Technology and economy
- `aera` is used in MK147 as a conscious transductive expression domain/network; keep this local meaning separate from the **AERA** framework title until the author explicitly reconciles names.
- PSSP = projected/manifold scaffold identity used for Shaping. `maege glass`, Genesis constructs, collectible Genesis Cards, `raeon` and corridor infrastructure are already present in AERA as available examples.
- MK147 currency labels: `eln` bank notes and `ahm` coins; anti-counterfeiting specifications remain open. Do not make this a universal AERA currency.
- **Source-specific diamond density ladder**: `13R=1O; 11O=1Y; 7Y=1G; 5G=1B; 3B=1V; 2V=1UV`. This is in MK147's compact `DIAMONDS_CURRENCY_EQUIPMENT.md` but not the supplied seven-page Genesis Diamond master. **Do not silently integrate into the definitive v0.6.0 diamond specification**; preserve as a source discrepancy for author review.
- **Ring radius wording discrepancy:** MK147 compact source calls Red 3ft, Orange 5ft, Yellow 7ft, Green 11ft, Blue 17ft, Violet 23ft, White 31+ft `LOCKED`; the definitive 20-page ring master says exact values remain adjustable. Preserve the master as primary, flag for author review; do not silently change either.
