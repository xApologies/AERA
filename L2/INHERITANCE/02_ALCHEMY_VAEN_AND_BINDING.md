# MK147 source inheritance: Alchemy, vaen, and Binding

**Scope:** An available AERA implementation. Do not promote MK147 Binding Circles to the universal shaping interface.

## Material-science distinctions
- Ordinary Type-1 matter remains useful; Genesis-infused **ordinary** material is not automatically a newly discovered higher-order Type-2 element.
- Alchemists supply material stock to blacksmiths and industry, including enhanced iron with strength, durability and corrosion resistance; they also prepare nutrient media, beverages, potions, reactive mixtures and higher-order materials.
- Type 1 = ordinary periodic table; Type 2 ≈80% discovered, first-order Genesis-material processing; Type 3 ≈40% discovered, may combine Type 1+2 or Type 2+2; Type 4 ≈10–20%; Type 5 = frontier with one or two known examples. Discovery percentages are author approximations.
- Helium-4 + iron + Green Genesis Dust + sigil-guided Transduction → stable liquid-iron-like state is an **illustrative fictional recipe**, not a universal chemistry law.
- Awakening potions are **not MK147 canon**; this source uses Binding Circles.

## vaen and identity selection
- `vaen` is a naturally occurring Genesis-relevant **identity-bearing** specialty resource, not simply any high-energy material or another word for element. Examples: Voidbloom Lotus; Teardrop of the Everlasting Star.
- A vaen may carry **multiple identities**; a Binding can select one or more, not necessarily consume the complete source identity. Other suitable identity-bearing sources are possible.
- Binding Circle = large specialized sigil and identity-resolution scaffold. Inputs → identity selection → collapse/resolution → target's occupied identity/state. Targets may be people **or objects**.
- Binding acquisition and active Shaping are distinct. A Bound manifold may be deployed via PSSP, with exact stored-structure/projected-manifold terminology still OPEN.
- Fatigue is physiological/endurance-like reduced access, **not mana**; training improves endurance without eliminating fatigue or automatically upgrading a Binding.
