# MK147 source inheritance: physical sigils, industry, and Architect velis

**Status:** Source-derived expansion of *available* AERA technologies, not new universal requirements. MK147 `main` pinned at `ae7c8b406370b752fd5e6f809be0148315294379`.

## Sigil distinctions
- `ru’ne`: discrete physically inscribed, strongly orthogonal geometric component.
- Sigil: complete physical ru’ne structure **on a substrate**.
- Array: coordinated multiple physical sigils with system-scale effect.
- Activation: manual, continuous, conditional or environmental. Ambient-light-sensitive night lighting is an established example.
- Longevity principally follows physical substrate and inscription integrity; maintenance repairs wear. No generic universal lifetime.
- Industrial uses: water, sanitation/dissolution, lighting, barriers, grown materials, maege-glass manufacturing, metropolitan Genesis stabilization and prepared equipment. Mature civilizations can grow materials rather than exclusively mine them.

## Architects and velis
- An Architect directly forms/constrains Genesis-field topology; this does not require a physical inscription.
- `velis`: temporary local topology/anchor formed by an Architect, not a sigil.
- Persistence is coherence- and load-sensitive. Lightly used examples may last decades or centuries; heavy activation can unravel the field sooner. Architects may maintain, reinforce, restore or replace it. No universal timer.
- Agricultural velis support accelerated growth and nutrient uptake with suitable Alchemist-enriched soil; defensive velis create maintenance burdens through repeated use.
- **OPEN:** exact routine civic sigil trade/profession; do not assert Architects exclusively perform all sigil engineering.
