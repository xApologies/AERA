# Four standardized enchantment configurations

These are **property/operation configurations**, NOT four distinct energy basins, separate magical schools or mutually exclusive manufacture methods.

## 1. Intrinsic
A completed modification permanently changes the item's Genesis identity, e.g. an iron item's reduced effective mass. The new property needs no continuing activation by default. Its relationship to gravity/inertia is open.

## 2. Activated
The object's identity permanently contains an **activation-dependent capability**. Touching a hilt sigil, pressing a thumb, or supplying a conscious energy input may turn on a flaming-edge effect. The activation interface is author-selected. The effect can cease while the identity modification remains.

## 3. Finite reservoir — corrected wording
**The enchanted object draws energy from a finite reserve of either a Red-through-Violet Genesis crystal or a charged Genesis Diamond.** The reserve may be embedded, attached, coupled or otherwise connected by a compatible engineered arrangement. Energy drains when used; depletion ends or limits powered expression **without erasing the enchantment**. No automatic assumption that only diamonds are batteries or that every Genesis crystal behaves identically to a diamond.

## 4. Chromatic conduit
One or more suitable Genesis crystals act as chromatic **basin anchors/conduits**, establishing an item's connection to the required R/O/Y/G/B/V energy structures. Example: Red, Orange and Green crystals configured to support a fictional flaming edge. A conduit is not automatically a finite stored reserve; actual energy delivery, stability and throughput require valid geometry and rules.

## Composability
An enchanted sword may simultaneously have an intrinsic reduced-mass property, a touch activation, a finite reservoir and chromatic conduits. Each **coherent enchantment system** requires appropriate fabrication and resources; do not assume four properties necessarily cost exactly four activations or one.

## Optional material fatigue
An author may model local material Genesis-structure stress, cooldown, maintenance or reduced throughput after sustained activation. This is optional and must not automatically erase the permanently modified identity. No quantitative fatigue law is supplied.
