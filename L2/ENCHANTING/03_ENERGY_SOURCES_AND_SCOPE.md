# Source reconciliation — Genesis Diamonds, chromatic crystals and identity resources

**Genesis Diamond:** carbon-lattice energy **container/battery**; diamond remains diamond and energy remains energy. Any of six basin energies may be stored. Stored occupancy is organized to stable average energy density. Apparent charge-state color is **not necessarily source-basin identity**. Empty, infrared, R/O/Y/G/B/V and ultraviolet describe charging/average-density states, not extra chromatic basins. See `L2/TECHNOLOGY/GENESIS_DIAMONDS.md` and its original seven-page master.

**Genesis Crystal:** Red-through-Violet crystal material with basin-relevant identity and energy properties; may be used as a finite reserve in a designed enchantment **or** as a chromatic conduit/anchor. These are distinct engineered functions. Do not claim all crystals are diamonds or that crystals necessarily require identical charging rules.

**Genesis dust:** processed crystalline resource for fabrication, scaffolding or identity modification.

**`vaen`:** naturally occurring Genesis-relevant identity-bearing specialty resource, not merely high-energy material. One `vaen` may contain multiple admissible identities; the Binding operation may select a subset.
