# Object Genesis identity and enchanting manufacture

## Target
A sword, block, suit of armor or other construct has an object-local Genesis-field configuration with identity, inheritance, topology and material properties. Enchanting resolves a new admissible configuration into that object's persistent identity.

**Example:** one-foot iron cube, or a very large iron sword, receives an effective mass-reduction property. This is a **fictional** Genesis property change. No universal mass/inertia/gravity/conservation equation has been derived. Do not silently equate lower weight with lower inertial mass or claim ordinary iron spontaneously loses its physical matter. Precise semantics remain open.

## Prepared sigil method
- Prepare a physically realized, geometrically valid `ru’ne`/sigil structure on a substrate; use appropriate object anchor(s).
- Position the object in the designated location; provide identity-bearing `vaen`, chromatic Genesis crystals/dust and other designed resources.
- Activate the prepared field/Binding geometry; resolve one specified coherent identity modification; designated resources are consumed.
- Object retains the new Genesis identity when the sigil operation ends.

The resource set belongs to **one activation of one coherent enchantment system**, not necessarily one microscopic physical sub-property. Multiple independent enchantments normally require distinct operations/resource groups. Exact recipes, costs and item limits are author-defined.

## Direct Architect method
A sufficiently trained Architect accesses the object's local field, consciously constructs an admissible topology, stabilizes it, organizes necessary energy, resolves the identity modification and disengages. No prepared consumable sigil-material recipe is intrinsically required; the practitioner still expends effort/energy and may fail. Direct work is labor-intensive and can produce unusual one-off effects.

Both paths use the established six-stage Transduction lifecycle where conscious Transduction is involved: Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release. Binding acquisition and later operation are separate where applicable.

## Nonclaims
No mandatory item enchantment slot count, infinite masslessness, automatic destruction immunity, universal gem socket, power equation, or fixed prices. Complex effects must still be admissible and sufficiently engineered.
