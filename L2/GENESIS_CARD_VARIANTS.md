# AERA — Genesis Card Variants (Current Canon)

**Status:** Established AERA author decision, 2026-10-13. **Scope:** Layer Two technology/culture crosswalk to Layer One operational interface. This is a clarification, not a new Transduction system.

AERA permits **two variations of Genesis cards**:

1. **Collectible-only Genesis cards.** Cards created or held as collectibles, trading cards, cultural artifacts, or displays of encoded/depicted Genesis manifolds. Possession does **not** itself imply that the card can activate a Shaping.
2. **Operational Genesis cards.** Cards with encoded Genesis topology that can be activated as Transduction equipment under `L1/INTERFACES/03_GENESIS_CARDS.md`. These cards **can also be collected, traded, displayed, and valued as trading cards**, regardless of whether their current owner uses their activation capability. Collectibility and operational function are not mutually exclusive.

The distinction concerns **whether a card can perform operational Transduction**, not whether it is collectible. A collectible-only card is not automatically upgraded into an operational card by being traded or displayed. An operational card does not lose its encoded architecture when treated as a collectible.

**Operational constraints remain unchanged:** conscious Genesis coupling; embedded manifold; required chromatic basins anchored exclusively across all the practitioner's interfaces while the card is active; energy and fatigue; one active conduit per occupied basin; no arbitrary extra lifecycle stage. No new card capacity, price, rarity, upgrade, or activation rule is established here.

**Separate system:** `raeon` cards are a distinct tabletop/game use of maege-glass card technology, not automatically operational spellcasting Genesis cards. An author may choose a world with only collectible cards, operational cards, both, or neither; where operational cards exist, they may be collected.

**Author-level terminology:** 'trading card' describes use and value, not a third Genesis-card technology class. Existing MK147, MK727 and other story-specific collections remain their own source authority.
