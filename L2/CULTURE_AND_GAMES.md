# Available social and cultural systems — raeon, Prism, `aithren`, `vaelum`

These are documented cultural systems from MK147/MK157 and independent `_raeon`; an author may inherit them without creating a new game or civilization, but is not obliged to do so.

## `raeon` — mathematically defined field card game

**Author-chosen name** lower-case `raeon`; the video game project is [`_raeon`](https://github.com/xApologies/_raeon), with independent game rules and authority. Physical **maege-glass** cards support actively linked local field topology: Field Generator cards, Prime Fields, Utilities; geometric configuration, colored basin effects, Prime charge, restore/degrade and other interactions are governed by game's own accepted rules.

At the last publicly reviewed game state in this conversation, the current Cycle 1 lists **120 Field Generators, 14 Prime Fields, 51 Utilities = 185 ordinary identities**, with 60-card constructed decks and three active Prime positions; check source freshness before citing counts in future publications. The original earlier MK narrative listed a different draft count; current `_raeon` data wins for Raeon design. Preproduction software runtime is not assumed completed. The board picture in `visuals/` illustrates field links; it is **not** a rules proof.

Game developer source: [`data/cycles/cycle_01/card-system.json`](https://github.com/xApologies/_raeon/blob/main/data/cycles/cycle_01/card-system.json). Do not rewrite `_raeon`'s own governance or replace its repo with an AERA fork.

## Genesis Cards

Distinct culturally collectible cards representing bounded manifolds, concept art/geometry and Shaping identities, e.g., fireballs. People may collect/trade the cards without activating a Shaping. Different from the **Raeon playable field game** and different from future author-proposed **card-as-Shaping interface**.

## Prism — transduction-based sport

Established sport in MK157's cultural examples, similar in narrative role to a society's football/soccer. Can drive rivalries, fans, talent, clubs and stories; the full accepted competition rulebook belongs to its upstream source. AERA provides its existence as an available example, not automatic adoption or invented detailed sport mechanics.

## `aithren` community identity

Lowercase `aithren` is a participatory professional/social culture, not merely an adventurer-guild job. Dungeon delvers live around persistent, mappable/staged dangerous locations and form relaxed festival-like communities, technical knowledge exchange and friendships; Genesis storm chasers operate in transient high-risk domai/storm conditions harvesting dust/resources and handling operational logistics. Source author analogies include 1980s computer engineering, NASCAR infield social belonging, forward-operating-base competence and tornado chasers crossed with elite field operators. These analogies do **not** import cars or military ranks into the world. ER Foam circles support dialogue/information exchange. Cultural respect follows participation, discovery, competence and contribution.

## `vaelum` institutions and Accord Registry

Lowercase `vaelum` is an independently organized, formally recognized, contract-capable institution under which `aithren` may operate. Crown→Accord Registry→recognized vaelum→aithren describes **recognition, not universal military command**. Work includes monster work, dungeon/dmai excursions, escorts, research support, engineering, medical/agricultural service, security, transport and harvesting. Tax/property benefits may exist in a specific Crown system; rates, laws and governance are author-specific. Do not force another AERA civilization to have a Crown, an Accord or `vaelum`.
