# Environmental Genesis mechanisms — ELDRIS, dungeons, storms and domai

**These are optional world-environment systems permitted by the AERA Genesis framework.** Choosing a constrained equilibrium Starscape avoids having to write a monster crisis. Conversely, unstable neon structures provide a common fictional environmental explanation for suitable Genesis collapse. No universal numerical neon threshold is declared.

## ELDRIS — stabilized recursive-collapse entities

An **eldris** is a persistent, bounded entity emerging when a local Genesis/chirality instability propagates recursively and becomes enclosed by closure rather than dispersing. The collapse stabilizes; it does not simply end. Its black wispy/rootlike/shadowy body is containment of collapse; limited colored leakage marks the **dominant basin identity** (e.g. eyes, wounds, attack traces). This is **not** the Black neon spectrum of Kira. It is not a demon, ordinary beast, or automatically a dungeon-only organism.

| ELDRIS basin-dominant family | Characteristic observed behavior | Broad threat category in source |
|---|---|---|
| Red | physical body-force, claws, teeth, contact | A |
| Orange | contact plus energy intrusion | A |
| Yellow | ranged energy-mass/force projection (beams, orbs) | A |
| Green | resonance-reinforced, enduring juggernaut | B |
| Blue | expressed force projection with explosions/secondary effects | B |
| Violet | high Genesis-like complex manifestation, legendary danger | C |

**ELDRIS are a narrative environmental pressure** and source of Genesis dust/resources for specialist work. The source categories are NOT character ranks and are not universal color→Transduction operation assignments. ## Genesis storms

Localized Genesis-field disturbances raising risk of unstable recursive collapse. A storm may create ELDRIS without creating a domai. The author chooses frequency and impact; absence of these events in a stable world is a legitimate setting choice.

## `domai`

Temporary, bounded Genesis-event **environment**, not a conscious lifeform. It may arise from suitable bounded storm/collapse; Red-through-Violet types (no White domai in accepted source model). Internal geometry may be non-isometric; depth/reach become unreliable across long distances. Core = **non-conscious superposed White Genesis Crystal**. Core destruction forces **progressive unraveling**, permitting risky final resource extraction and evacuation, not instantaneous disappearance. Domai may contain eldris, Genesis crystals, Genesis dust/White dust, `vaen` and other resources. Exact lifetime, size and encounter rules are author/world-specific.

## Dungeons — related but distinct

Persistent geographic environmental **prisms/strata** separating Genesis-field expression by the R→V structural basins. They are not just cleared 'levels' and need not vanish once explored. In MK147, compatible eldris tend to stay in their dungeon strata so settlements outside dungeon entrances are comparatively safe. **Dungeons can be imagined without an overarching disequilibrium and without ELDRIS** in an author's AERA setting. Their local architecture, strata and external cultures are author-defined.

## World example — balance and monster pressure

MK231 inherits an unusually displaced high Yellow neon singularity as a Starscape-local mechanism promoting collapse/eldris/storms/domai. A settled equilibrium world might avoid that entirely, while still supporting dungeons, Alchemy, Genesis flora and human culture. **Do not universalize the MK231 Yellow defect** as the reason all monsters in all AERA worlds exist.
