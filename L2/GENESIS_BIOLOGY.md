# Genesis flora, fauna and biological variation

AERA doesn't restrict the types of flora/fauna, nor require anthropomorphic or fantasy races. Biology is author-defined within the chosen environment and the Genesis Field's overall existence model.

## Three existing organism classifications

1. **Non-Genesis:** life functioning without specialized Genesis-dependent biological capabilities (despite existing within a Genesis-based Starscape).
2. **Genesis-adapted:** life that benefits from Genesis interaction for additional properties but can survive without that benefit. Examples: denser plant tissues, unusual luminescence, altered growth, structures recognizing local energy.
3. **Genesis-dependent:** life whose survival/development depends on an active Genesis-field interaction; its physiology eventually fails without suitable Genesis conditions.

The same classifications can be applied to flora and fauna. **Glowing does not automatically mean Genesis-dependent**, and a Genesis-active organism does not have to glow.

## Explicit source-of-inspiration visuals and resources

The thread supplied images of blue/pink bioluminescent forests and imagined a patch of mushrooms with luminous Red/Orange Genesis channels circulating inside the tissues. These are **visual and biological examples**, not locked species. A fungal patch may have elevated local Genesis concentration, become a material-science input to an alchemist and, **only if carrying a suitable Genesis-relevant identity**, qualify as `vaen` for a Binding. High Genesis energy alone is NOT the sufficient definition of `vaen`.

## Life, Shaping, morphology

Dragons, Unicorns and other fantasy creatures can be biological expressions of the author's Starscape, not necessarily demonic or illusory. The MK231 chosen chromatic native populations are Dragon (Red), Phoenix (Orange), Primordial (Yellow), Kitsune (Green), Leviathan (Blue), Unicorn (Violet), with human biology in White. These are **not mandatory AERA species**. Some chromatic races have historically learned humanoid form for diplomacy in White. Unconscious/instinctive biological Genesis behavior is possible in fiction even where humans operate through fully conscious Transduction.

## Humans: a particular fantasy model

A selected author-level model groups dwarf-like, ordinary-human-like and elf-like forms as differentiated **human physiological expressions** (one underlying humanity, not independent magical races). Evolution, gravity, skull/brain physiology, neuroplastic development and cognition can affect how these groups differ. **Do not hard-code worth, profession, intelligence level or magical ability from species label**. An elf is not inherently able to cast fireballs and a dwarf is not inherently a smith. Humans of other planets may lack conscious Genesis coupling entirely.

## Interactions with Alchemy

Plant and animal materials can enter the material supply chain; physical chemistry and Genesis-relevant resource identities are separately relevant. Distinguish Type 1–5 alchemical material classification from simple plant taxonomy. These depend on the author-selected setting and are not pre-populated catalogues for all Starscapes.
