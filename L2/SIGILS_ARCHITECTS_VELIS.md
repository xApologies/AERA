# `ru’ne`, sigils, Architects and `velis`

**Source:** MK147 dedicated current system files. These are established AERA-compatible Genesis technologies but AERA does not demand that every civilization invent or practice them.

## Physical sigils

- **`ru’ne`** — individual physically inscribed, strongly orthogonal geometric mark/component of a sigil.
- **Sigil** — complete formalized physically realized `ru’ne` structure on a substrate; supports conditional, manual or continuous operations.
- **Sigil array** — multiple coordinated sigils whose collective geometry/topology yields system-scale Transduction/technology.

Physical substrate integrity matters: durable sigils may last as long as substrate and inscription geometry remain intact; damage and geometry wear require repair. Sigils may be grown/formed into stone, equipment, lighting surfaces or industrial manufacturing systems, not merely mystical decoration.

**Applications in MK147/Genesis Point** include city-scale Genesis-field stabilization, safe road/settlement infrastructure, sanitation/water transport, lighting, barriers, growth of materials/stone/glass, and engineered buildings. The author has historical Genesis Point examples of vast city-disc construction from a sigil-encoded seed/pylon; exact former math and dimensions need source recovery (see `L0/GRAVITY_TIME_ER_FOAM.md`).

## Architects and `velis`

An **Architect** is the current working professional label for a practitioner who interacts with the Genesis Field *directly*, arranging/constraining its local topology, manifolds, boundaries and geometry to establish persistent effects. **An Architect does not have to place a conventional physical sigil for every field interaction.** The eventual title can be changed by the author, but the functional identity is established.

A **`velis`** is a *temporary, localized Genesis-field topology* formed by an Architect: an 'anchor' in author/workshop vocabulary. It is **not** the same object as `ru’ne` or a physical sigil. Its persistence depends on topological coherence; low-load velis could endure long times (even decades/centuries), heavy use shortens effective life and maintenance/reinforcement restores it. No universal timer.

### Agricultural example

Architect projects a local growth field across a specified region/footprint; nutrients come from Alchemist-made enriched soil. For a **suitable crop under suitable conditions**, one growing season may produce seven harvests rather than one. This is an established **world example**, not a rule that any seed magically yields seven harvests. Explains abundance of plant food in a monster-pressure civilization; other worlds choose different conditions.

### City example

MK147's large cities can use coordinated major sigils to suppress Genesis-collapse risk across a much larger territory than the urban footprint, permitting highways, farms, settlements and trade. Exact radius/corner configuration is a **world-specific source example**, not universally mandated.
