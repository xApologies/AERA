# Genesis-compatible technologies — available catalogue

**Available AERA technologies**, not mandatory objects in every era. A medieval/preindustrial civilization may use simple sigil lighting; an advanced interplanetary one may use Genesis-embedded information devices. The same underlying Genesis Field/Transduction vocabulary applies, but not every possible device has detailed source implementation specifications.

## `maege glass`

Genesis-interactive, Genesis-reinforced glass material whose physical form factor can be changed: 2.5×3-inch card, handheld, 13-inch tablet, architectural panels, etc. User describes high durability comparable with advanced reinforced materials, not literally guaranteed indestructibility. A localized Genesis structure/embedded system may be transferred/'flashed' into the manufactured substrate by engineered sigil tooling; the end user activates the interface without understanding the mathematics, analogous to a consumer using a smartphone without chip expertise.

**Uses:** Raeon Field Generator/Prime/Utility cards; collectible Genesis cards; image/video recording/display; touch controls; variable opacity walls; larger holographic/resolution-like interfaces; possible Genesis-based computation and data storage. Some advanced computer-like implementations are remembered from Genesis Point, but exact architectures remain **source-recovery** unless directly sourced. Manufacturing glass and its embedded designs at industrial scale is accepted as a world technology possibility, not an obligation for every fantasy civilization.

## Raeon cards ≠ spellcasting cards

Raeon cards are designed for an **in-world tabletop field game**. Their field generators organize local geometric/topological relations, color/charge moves around the game field, Prime Fields take charge, and Utilities affect the accepted rules. Physical maege glass is the technology substrate. The *video game implementation* remains an independent project; AERA can use the cultural/technological game without importing game runtime mechanics. **Card-based Shaping interfaces** remain a different, prospective author project.

## Genesis Cards

Collectible maege-glass items containing/displaying bounded Genesis manifold or Shaping representations (fireballs, structures, etc.), traded for cultural/collectible value. They are **not automatically castable spells**. MK147/MK157 example characters collect them separately from Raeon playable cards.

## Genesis diamonds and energy storage

MK147 defines carbon/diamond-like lattice energy-storage technology, sizes ranging from small chips to large infrastructure stores, with chromatic density appearances and other world-specific metrics. Those charging times/ratios are **example engineering**, not universal Genesis mathematics. Optional for AERA civilizations.

## Dimensional rings

MK147's dimensional ring is a topology/inheritance reassignment-based storage technology rather than an arbitrary pocket universe. Source-defined staging concepts include storage Expand→Select→Collapse→Conserve and retrieval Recognize→Select→Collapse→Retrieve. Exact radii and user skill bounds belong to the original source implementation.

## ER Foam

**PLACEHOLDER ONLY:** Einstein–Rosen Foam, a Genesis-field projection used as a **tactile surface**. Originates in MK Ultra gravity research. Do not reconstruct or imagine its math. Narrative reported uses: projected roads, responsive touches/buttons, maps with depth. See L0 placeholder.

## Mass manufacture and intellectual property

Sigil and material engineering can support reproducible products from hidden proprietary mathematical designs. Example: a company holds its Raeon field/topology mathematics privately and flashes manufactured blank cards with prepared systems. A world might legally protect applied Genesis-math constructions, manufacturing designs and specialty sigils, with royalties/foundations/institutions; precise legal rules are **author-defined**, not modern patent statutes copied verbatim.
