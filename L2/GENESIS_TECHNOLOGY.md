# Genesis-compatible technologies — available catalogue

**Available AERA technologies**, not mandatory objects in every era. A medieval/preindustrial civilization may use simple sigil lighting; an advanced interplanetary one may use Genesis-embedded information devices. The same underlying Genesis Field/Transduction vocabulary applies, but not every possible device has detailed source implementation specifications.

## `maege glass`

Genesis-interactive, Genesis-reinforced glass material whose physical form factor can be changed: 2.5×3-inch card, handheld, 13-inch tablet, architectural panels, etc. User describes high durability comparable with advanced reinforced materials, not literally guaranteed indestructibility. A localized Genesis structure/embedded system may be transferred/'flashed' into the manufactured substrate by engineered sigil tooling; the end user activates the interface without understanding the mathematics, analogous to a consumer using a smartphone without chip expertise.

**Uses:** Raeon Field Generator/Prime/Utility cards; collectible Genesis cards; image/video recording/display; touch controls; variable opacity walls; larger holographic/resolution-like interfaces; possible Genesis-based computation and data storage. Some advanced computer-like implementations are remembered from Genesis Point, but exact architectures remain **source-recovery** unless directly sourced. Manufacturing glass and its embedded designs at industrial scale is accepted as a world technology possibility, not an obligation for every fantasy civilization.

## Raeon cards ≠ spellcasting cards

Raeon cards are designed for an **in-world tabletop field game**. Their field generators organize local geometric/topological relations, color/charge moves around the game field, Prime Fields take charge, and Utilities affect the accepted rules. Physical maege glass is the technology substrate. The *video game implementation* remains an independent project; AERA can use the cultural/technological game without importing game runtime mechanics. **Operational Genesis Cards** are a separate Transduction interface; Raeon cards do not automatically acquire that function.

## Genesis Cards

Two [Genesis Card variations](GENESIS_CARD_VARIANTS.md) are available: **collectible-only cards**, containing/displaying bounded Genesis manifold or Shaping representations (fireballs, structures, etc.), and **operational cards**, whose encoded topology supports Transduction under the [existing interface rules](../L1/INTERFACES/03_GENESIS_CARDS.md). **Operational cards can also be collected, traded and displayed**; collectible use does not remove their encoded architecture, and trading a collectible-only card does not make it operational. Possession alone does not imply a castable spell. MK147/MK157 example characters' collections remain separate from Raeon playable cards and retain their own source authority. Authors may adopt either variation, both or neither; no trading mechanics are prescribed.

Under the [current `aen` clarification](../L1/NEUROGENESIS/03_AEN_TRAINING_AND_FATIGUE.md), operational Cards draw through their exclusive basin conduits and receive **no `aen` throughput boost**. Card-occupied basins remain unavailable to that practitioner's `aen`; all use still contributes to cumulative basin-specific fatigue.

## Genesis diamonds and energy storage

Genesis Diamonds are stable carbon-lattice containers for stored Genesis energy; the diamond and energy remain distinct. Charge-state appearance describes average density, with nine states from empty through ultraviolet. The source supplies six reference charging times, infrastructure charging and four size classes as optional world-engine examples. See [Genesis Diamonds](TECHNOLOGY/GENESIS_DIAMONDS.md) and its preserved seven-page master; no universal capacity equation is introduced.

## Dimensional rings

Dimensional Rings conserve recursive topology through inheritance reassignment rather than a pocket dimension. Storage remains Expand→Select→Collapse→Conserve; retrieval remains Recognize→Select→Collapse→Retrieve. Conscious recognition and user familiarity govern retrieval without an inventory UI; reach values are illustrative source-device references. See [Dimensional Rings](TECHNOLOGY/DIMENSIONAL_RINGS.md) and its locked 20-page master, including the Jenkins principle. This is an available implementation, not a mandatory AERA device.

## Enchanting — persistent object-identity engineering

[Enchanting architecture](ENCHANTING/00_READ_FIRST.md) describes an optional author-level system for persistent object-identity modification. Prepared sigil/miniature Binding Circle fabrication consumes a resource group per coherent enchantment system; direct Architect fabrication uses conscious manifold construction without inherently requiring the same specialized consumables. Both require admissible engineering and energy; the existing six-stage Transduction lifecycle remains.

Intrinsic, activated, finite-reservoir and chromatic-conduit configurations may be combined. A finite reserve can be **either a Red-through-Violet Genesis crystal or a charged Genesis Diamond**; a conduit is not automatically a stored reserve. An effect stopping or a reserve emptying does not erase the modified identity. Diamond charge-state colors and Ring inheritance-reassignment mechanics retain their own definitions. Material stress/cooldown is optional; mass/inertia laws and interaction with personal Genesis Card anchors remain OPEN. See [source distinctions](ENCHANTING/03_ENERGY_SOURCES_AND_SCOPE.md) and [authority/open questions](ENCHANTING/04_AUTHORITY_AND_OPEN.md).

## Mass manufacture and intellectual property

Sigil and material engineering can support reproducible products from hidden proprietary mathematical designs. Example: a company holds its Raeon field/topology mathematics privately and flashes manufactured blank cards with prepared systems. A world might legally protect applied Genesis-math constructions, manufacturing designs and specialty sigils, with royalties/foundations/institutions; precise legal rules are **author-defined**, not modern patent statutes copied verbatim.
