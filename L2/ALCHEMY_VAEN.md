# Alchemy — Genesis material science and `vaen`

Alchemy in the AERA framework is **material science**, not merely potion-brewing. It inherits ordinary chemistry as Type 1 materials and allows additional Genesis-mediated higher-order materials. Alchemists can supply manufacturers, blacksmiths, farmers, healers and Binding practitioners as well as develop foods, potions, reactive preparations, Genesis-infused stock materials and novel stable states. The original MK147 material taxonomy is **available AERA capability**, not a fixed set of novel elements invented in this packet.

## Five material types — discovered fraction author approximations

| Type | Current status/definition | Discovery status within fictional model |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | ordinary chemical periodic table of elements and conventional material science | established real chemistry reference |
| **2** | first-order Genesis-material interaction / novel stable state using Type-1 matter with appropriate Genesis energy/dust and sigil-guided Transduction | ~80% discovered |
| **3** | higher-order materials; may use Type 1+2 or Type 2+2 and appropriate Genesis processing | ~40% discovered |
| **4** | more advanced Genesis material category | ~10–20% discovered |
| **5** | frontier category | only one or two confirmed fictional examples; category exists |

**The 80/40/10–20 figures describe the *setting's estimated extent of discovery*, not a claim that real periodic table chemistry is incomplete.** Authors can add new Type 2–5 materials with compatible properties; they do not have to pre-populate all tables. Type 2–5 are *not* four more physical periodic tables automatically understood at a mathematical theorem level.

**Source illustration:** Helium-4 + iron + Green Genesis Dust under suitable sigil/Transduction conditions yielding a stable liquid-iron-like Type-2 material is a fictional recipe example from MK147, not ordinary real-world chemistry and not a universal recipe law.

## `vaen` — separate classification

`vaen` is lower-case invented AERA vocabulary for naturally occurring specialty resources having unusual **Genesis-relevant identities/properties**. Examples from MK147: *Voidbloom Lotus*, *Teardrop of the Everlasting Star*. A `vaen` may be found in the Wilds, dungeon resources, domai, unusual plants/fungi and other appropriate environments; multiple identities may be present within one source. A `vaen` is **not automatically a chemical element**, nor is it simply any material with a high energy concentration. The term replaces vague 'fantasy element' for special identity-bearing resources while 'element' retains its material-science meaning.

## Binding use (optional shaping interface)

A prepared Binding Circle, a large structured sigil and Genesis identity-resolution scaffold, can select compatible identity/identities from `vaen` or other identity-bearing inputs and resolve them into a target's occupied identity/state. Source resources might be extremely rare and economically valuable for many applications. **This is a particular MK147/MK231 implementation, not the required universal Transduction interface.**

## Agriculture/technology

Alchemy can make enriched growing media supporting accelerated growth under an Architect's `velis`. Manufacturing can introduce durable/corrosion-resistant Genesis-infused iron stock, higher-order glass composites, and other designs. Specific prices, availability, extraction and trade belong to the author/world.

## Two successive natural events · November 8, 2026

**Established for natural vaen.**

**Native White object/material → first Genesis expression → second identity-bearing vaen realization.** The first event is a field-mediated transformation, biological evolution/adaptation in biological cases, or analogous geological/material reorganization in nonliving cases. The second event is a distinct Genesis interaction with environmental geometry/pressure, establishing **specialized identity-bearing geometry** within the entire item or a particular component. Genesis expression alone is insufficient. A glowing fungus is not necessarily vaen. The identity need not be globally unique, extremely rare, exclusively Green, or a large energy reservoir.

Examples: special Red Arc Awakening mushroom; flowers; a patch of Genesis-expressive fungi with one/two mushrooms becoming vaen; specialized environmentally formed seeds; trees (whole tree or bark, leaves, resin, sap/amber); Genesis-expressive deer with identity-bearing antlers; optional shells; mineral nodules, deposits and stones (Orange Arc moss ecology); Genesis-interacting lightning-formed glass; conditional fossils/mineralized wood. Lichen remains a preferred example of Stage-1 expression rather than selected vaen. Previously excluded antlers and fossils are now conditional possibilities. The frog in Orange Arc is Genesis-expressive fauna, **not vaen**. Not every substrate must be alive, and not every expressed organism has a vaen component.

Biological expression may mean adaptation or dependence. Geological reorganization is not biological speciation. Whole items and components can qualify: a tree need not be excluded; antlers need not require the death of the deer. A tree’s leaves, bark, sap, resin or amber-like product can bear an identity distinct from its substrate. Ordinary roots are not prohibited. Optional shells, fossils and lightning-formed glass are possibilities, not automatic classes of vaen. The MK163 mushroom and Orange mineral examples are worked examples of this framework, not obligatory AERA story events. The original Voidbloom Lotus and Teardrop of the Everlasting Star examples, multi-identity use, optional Binding and Type 1–5 categories above are unchanged.
