# Starscapes, planetary environment, astronomy and geography

AERA's White material existence may include multiple worlds, planetary systems and distant cosmological structures within a Starscape. The six chromatic structures are always foundational energy organizations; whether they also form navigable realms with terrain/inhabitants is the author's choice. The author may place different worlds in the same Starscape, with Rainbow Road/corridors allowing cross-world transport **if that specific setting realizes the corresponding physical gateways**.

### MK147's circumbinary author example — NOT the AERA global astronomy law

The supplied MK147 orbital/Starscape source documents use a circumbinary star system: approximately 0.55 and 0.45 solar-mass stars, binary separation ~0.25 AU and period ~46 planetary days. Four cited worlds: an inner Ash World, temperate Kira's World, Frontier World and Great Giant outer planet. Kira's World uses a 31-hour day, 360 local-day year, four 90-day seasons and six-day weeks (60 weeks). The earlier 11-month calendar totals have unresolved mismatches across historical documents; **do not silently invent missing days**. All data are particular MK147 *fictional setting inputs*, not universal physics of AERA.

### Environmental engine author choices

- Orbital system: single star, binary, more stars, outer worlds, galaxy scale, etc.
- Planetary gravity, geochemistry, oceanic/atmospheric conditions, geological history, local radiation and environmental constraints.
- Continents, ridges, rivers, oceans, forests, caves, strange topologies.
- Ecological classification and whether organisms have Genesis-adapted/dependent biology.
- Whether physical chromatic-realm crossings exist and how portals are discovered/mapped.
- Local calendars and time correspondence (or none); AERA does not fix a universal numeric clock ratio.

Gravity/environment/evolution may inspire differences in local human physiology; this **does not establish a universal formula** assigning dwarves to a massive world or elves to a low-gravity planet.

### Nonordinary chromatic geometry (MK231 example)

An author's Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Violet realm may have nonordinary globally continuous topology, yet locally recognizable terrain (mountains, oceans, clouds, walkable paths). Its own local temporal continuity may pass differently from another realm. Multiple exits could lead to different White material planets. These are **optional MK231 realization details**, not compulsory properties of the R–V energy source for all authors.
