# Civilization Engine — available knowledge cultures, professions, economies

AERA does **not** fix a monarchy, guild system, industrial era, city plan or social class hierarchy. The author chooses whether their people live in kingdoms, tribes, city-states, future civilizations or different ecosystems. All of these belong to Layer Two World Engine, with necessary biological character decisions in L3.

## Institutions of Knowledge

There is always room for **organized transmission of Transduction knowledge** in AERA civilizations that practice magic. The author picks its form and curriculum, including institutions focused on concepts (Blackstone), direct Shape/Hold geometry, apprenticeship, philosophy or other subjects. MK147's Blackstone is a prestigious Institute specializing in knowledge of Chemistry, Topology, Differential Geometry, Biology, Genesis Field Theory and related science because conceptual knowledge improves Shaping efficiency.

## Mathematics as high-status social work

Genesis mathematics underlies real *in-world functional technologies* such as physical sigils, manufacturing, architects' topologies, Shaping and advanced devices. An author may make applied mathematicians culturally celebrated, affluent, central to institutions, research, commerce and licensed intellectual-property development. It's not mandatory that every AERA civilization grant math prestige; it's a powerful example from MK147/157. Applied mathematically defined Genesis constructions may be patented/licensed **under an author-defined legal regime**, not an automatic real-world legal claim about patenting abstract mathematics.

## Human social/professional labels

Common classifications (examples, not compulsory magic domains):

| Label | Focus |
|---|---|
| `maege` (singular), `maegi` (plural) | Broad Transduction/Shaping practice |
| `helkir` | Coherence-based healing/topological stabilization; a human professional and social role |
| Augmenter | Resonance-led enhancement/reinforcement |
| Summoner | Manifested entities, constructs and controlled Genesis projections |
| Architect | Direct Genesis-field geometry and persistent topological constructions (`velis`); title working |
| Alchemist | Material scientist working with Genesis-relevant chemistry/Type 1–5 materials |

These are **professions** not an exhaustive spell taxonomy; card wielders/tattooed casters are not automatically one of these.

## Living culture

Some worlds may emphasize mathematical games (Raeon), collectible Genesis cards, Genesis-based sport (Prism), coffee-equivalent `abecca`, ER Foam conversation circles, heroic monster expeditions, scientific institutions, charities, intellectual property, cuisine, guildlike independent organizations and richly textured personal routines. No individual choice is required in every AERA novel.

## MK147 selected setting example

Romanticized late seventeenth / eighteenth-century preindustrial human material culture: grown orthogonal stone blocks, sigil municipal infrastructure, water/greenery, abundant plant agriculture, dungeon support towns, provincial capital structure, Institutions of Knowledge, cultural card games. **This is an example**; not all future worlds are obligated to be preindustrial or to share its legal institutions.
