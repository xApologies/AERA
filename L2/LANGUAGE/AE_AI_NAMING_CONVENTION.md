# AERA — AE / AI Naming Convention

**Status:** Established optional linguistic inheritance.  
**Layer:** L2 — World Engine / Civilization / Language.  
**Scope:** Naming practices and some semantic vocabulary; **not** Genesis mathematics, a universal grammar, or a mandatory biological classification.

## Core convention

In an AERA-compatible naming tradition, the letter sequence **AE** is a general **masculine** personal-name convention, while **AI** is a general **feminine** personal-name convention. They are tendencies, **not restrictions**: names may deliberately cross these conventions, omit them, or use entirely different patterns. Authors may adopt, adapt, or decline this naming tradition without violating the AERA framework.

In certain inherited institutional or relational vocabulary, **AE** can signal a *possessive, holding, or organizational* relationship; **AI** can signal a *non-possessive, participating, or belonging* relationship. These are semantic tendencies in particular established terms, **not** universal operators that mechanically determine ownership, agency, or gender from spelling.

## Established examples and exact spellings

| Word | Spelling | Established use | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `vaelum` | V-A-E-L-U-M | Institution/organization with holding or organizational authority | AE relational example. Earlier spoken/transcribed variants such as `Velum` should not silently replace the documented form. |
| `aithren` | A-I-T-H-R-E-N | Adventurer/participant culture associated with the institution | AI relational example; association is not a claim that every individual belongs to one organization. |
| `maege` | M-A-E-G-E | Singular practitioner | Retain canonical spelling. |
| `maegi` | M-A-E-G-I | Plural practitioners | An established irregular/lexical plural; do **not** force a change to `maige` or infer gender from the plural spelling. |

**Author-provided counterexamples:** a son may be named `Vaine` (V-A-I-N-E), and a daughter `Faene` (F-A-E-N-E). Both are legitimate. Their spelling does not override the individual's identity.

## Use in name generation

For a request such as “generate fifty masculine names,” an author or assistant may favor the AE sequence; for “fifty feminine names,” favor AI. Avoid claiming that the letters alone establish gender or that all names must contain exactly one sequence. Generate pronounceable, varied names and respect the setting's established phonotactics and lexicon when available. Names proposed by an assistant remain **examples**, not canonical people or dictionary entries until approved.

## Distinctions and authority

1. **Optional worldbuilding convention:** it is part of AERA's available linguistic inheritance, not a mandatory L0/L1 rule.
2. **Personal names versus semantic vocabulary:** masculine/feminine naming and possessive/non-possessive institutional associations are related usages, not a proven universal equivalence.
3. **Existing canon controls:** never rename an existing character, profession, culture, or institution solely to satisfy the pattern.
4. **Languages remain separate:** this convention does not define the Genesis semantic/programming languages or AEVEA. Those are separate source-dependent linguistic domains to be added later.
5. **No biological determinism:** spelling does not prescribe identity, capabilities, social status, or magical access.
