# AERA — Aenith / Aineth / Ain lexicon

**Status:** Author-locked terminology, 2026-10-13. **Scope:** AERA reusable high-fantasy lexicon and object-level Genesis engineering. This document names existing mechanics; it does not replace them.

## Canonical spellings and referents

| Term | Spelling | Meaning | Relational role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Aenith** | A-E-N-I-T-H | An object that possesses one or more persistent enchantments | **AE**, possessive: the object owns the modification |
| **Aineth** | A-I-N-E-T-H | The process or discipline of enchanting, i.e. deliberately modifying an object's Genesis identity and topology | **AI**, non-possessive: the operation rather than the owner |
| **Ain** | A-I-N | An individual enchantment: the persistent identity/property modification resulting from Aineth | Result of Aineth, possessed by an Aenith |

**Author-level translation:** *enchanting* remains the familiar English category; *Aineth* is the in-world technical/process term. *Aenith* names the modified object, **not** the process. *Ain* names the modification, **not** the object. Capitalization in headings is editorial; lowercase usage in ordinary prose may follow the author's style.

**Canonical relationship:** Through **Aineth**, an **Ain** is established; the object that possesses it becomes an **Aenith**.

Example: a sword undergoes Aineth through a prepared sigil or direct Architect manipulation; its reduced-effective-mass property is an Ain; the sword is an Aenith. A sword can possess multiple Ain. The terminology applies equally to armor, tools, constructs, or other compatible objects.

## Linguistic inheritance
The AE/AI contrast is part of AERA's **optional linguistic naming convention**. Here AE marks the possessive relation of the modified object, whereas AI marks the non-possessive process/result. This lexical family may be understood as an archaic technical inheritance, but its precise historical etymology and full grammatical derivation are not established. Do **not** generalize this as a mandatory rule for all AERA languages or every AE/AI word.

Distinguish from:
- `aen`: Awakened cutaneous chromatic energy channels, **not** enchantments;
- `vaen`: identity-bearing specialty resources, **not** necessary for every Architect-performed Aineth;
- `ru’ne`: inscribed geometric elements comprising physical sigils;
- `maege` / `maegi`: established singular/plural spellings.

## Engineering preserved
Aineth operates by modifying an object's **persistent Genesis identity**. Two manufacture routes remain: (1) standardized, resource-consuming prepared sigil/Binding, and (2) direct, labor-intensive Architect modification without mandatory specialized consumables. Four **combinable** configurations remain: intrinsic property; activated property; finite reservoir supplied by **either a Red–Violet Genesis crystal or a charged Genesis Diamond**; and chromatic basin conduit. Deactivation or reserve depletion never automatically erases the Ain.
