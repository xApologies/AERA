# AERA — optional long-lived aristocracy
Layer Two optional worldbuilding, not a required AERA social system.

Expensive Genesis potions and White Awakening can create unequal access to longevity. Noble houses can have large *simultaneously living* family trees spanning many adult generations. The same elders may control estates, investments, infrastructure, institutions and political power for centuries; succession is delayed, while intergenerational accumulation can compound family wealth. Less wealthy families may face shorter accumulation horizons and fewer opportunities to buy biological enhancement. Economic effects are possibilities, not universal laws: taxation, mobility, access and government remain author-defined.

Awakening is a biological status, not magical profession or competence. Nobles may seek it for health, longer life, appearance, prestige or political marriages, even for someone who never forms an aen ring. Natural skin, hair and eye pigmentation remain. Beauty and marriage bargaining are *in-world social attitudes*, not universal truths. Awakened status, Transduction proficiency, wealth and political rank are independent.

An optional social practice: nobles may use Bindings routinely and operational Genesis Cards for rare, high-output one-off Shapings. Cards remain exclusive basin conduits, receive no aen throughput boost and still cause fatigue. Do not prescribe this equipment pattern to all cultures.
