# AERA — Chromatic Native Denizens: current optional realm realization

**Status:** Author-accepted AERA live-model update, 2026-10-13.
**Layer:** L2 (optional realm/population worldbuilding). L0 energy structures and L1 Transduction remain unchanged.

## Scope and rule
The six chromatic energy basins Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet are foundational energy structures, not intrinsically geographic places, races, or magic schools. White is their superposition, not an ordinary seventh chromatic basin. Authors can use them purely as energy conduits (as in MK147), or optionally realize them as navigable domains/realms with intelligent populations, environments and Starscape bridges. AERA supplies the following **default optional native populations for the inhabited-realm realization**. Authors may substitute other populations; no biological form is mandated by L0.

| Basin | Current default denizen | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Red | Dragons | Retained; large, physically powerful, flight-capable, six distinct operational specializations across population; each dragon has one native specialization per MK231 authority, not all six per individual. Further physical redesign pending. |
| Orange | **Vaelith** (V-A-E-L-I-T-H) | New default replacing Phoenix: living Genesis network, distributed coherence, regeneration, persistent shell; Coherence/Decoherence/Rebirth. |
| Yellow | Primordials | Retained; inorganic, organic, and **process** categories; living organized matter or process. |
| Green | **Sprites** | New default replacing Kitsune: living bounded Genesis recursions, Green-dominant, collective true biological realization and admissible potential. |
| Blue | Leviathans | Retained; immense throughput/endurance and scale, not necessarily aquatic. |
| Violet | Unicorns | Retained; Seven Foundations as coherent magical whole and prophetic tradition. |

**White:** Humanity's dwarf/human/elf expressions are story-level options in MK231, not an automatic additional chromatic species. 

## Reproductive framework
Chromatic-native populations may reproduce through compatible **energetic contribution from multiple individuals**, not necessarily sexual mating or binary parents. Dragons and Leviathans can produce eggs through pooled energies; a contribution may be communal (e.g. three or four dragons). The form of offspring (egg, cradle, crystal or other structure) is population-dependent. Do not impose ordinary human sex reproduction as a universal chromatic rule. The Vaelith's visual male/female-like morphs do **not** by themselves establish distinct species or mating mechanics; author's remark was exploratory. Exact taxonomy remains open.
