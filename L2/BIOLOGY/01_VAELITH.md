# Orange — Vaelith

**Spelling locked:** Vaelith (V-A-E-L-I-T-H). **Status:** selected Orange-native intelligent population for AERA's optional inhabited-realm realization, replacing Phoenix as default (not prohibiting Phoenix species).

## Four mutually dependent components
1. **Living filaments** — functional internal Genesis communication/energy-distribution network, not external spiderwebs or decorative ribbons.
2. **Distributed coherence nodes** — regulatory, networked structures coordinating integrity, energy and repair; not necessarily individually conscious.
3. **Branching and regeneration** — growing, rerouting and repairing pathways and damaged structures.
4. **Persistent identity and shell** — continuous individual identity sustained through distributed network and a physical energy shell, not one indispensable core.

Together: a **single living Genesis system** with an internally visible, neural-network-like architecture. Orange-native focus: **Coherence, complementary Decoherence, and Rebirth**. Exceptional healing/stabilization and reconstructive persistence; rebirth's threshold, identity retention and limits remain OPEN. Do not imply automatic immortality or Kira's extraordinary Black-level Decoherence in every Vaelith. Fire, light, avian flight and speculative EM attacks were Phoenix associations, **not** defining Vaelith capabilities.

## Visual canon
The selected visual direction is an elegant, tall, trim, minimally muscular, largely androgynous humanoid. Some examples appear male/female, but avoid imposing human sexual dimorphism or sexual reproduction as a necessary species mechanism. A continuous gray/charcoal, somewhat translucent **energy shell** allows faint glimpses of internal filaments, coherence nodes and branching. This shell is the body, **not armor plating, artificial exosuit or clothing**. It can suppress visible Genesis emission and appear subdued gray. No head ribbons/tendrils. Avoid overly curvy or voluptuous anatomy, exaggerated musculature, saturated uniform orange glow, or overt armored plates.

Orange is the dominant **energy structuring**, not a command to render the body uniformly orange. The illustrative 80/20 configuration allows other chromatic contributions internally; the multicolor *variant lineup* on the reference sheet is NOT accepted as separate canonical color variants. Preserve the author's accepted general morphology and restrained visible internal network; exact exterior chemistry/opacity remains an author-level open parameter.

## Energetic reproduction
As with other chromatic-native populations, energetic contribution creates a new individual; exact Vaelith developmental form and contributors are OPEN. Distinguish this from repair/rebirth of an existing individual.
