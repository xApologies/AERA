# Green — Sprites

**Status:** author-established Green-native intelligent population in optional inhabited-realm AERA, replacing Kitsune as default; Kitsune remain optional. **Core mechanism:** bounded, resolved, persistent **Genesis recursion** sharing a recursion/containment mechanism with ELDRIS, but Sprites are naturally occurring intelligent living Genesis entities, not automatically ELDRIS, collapse monsters, or inherently hostile.

## Individual natural form
A small candle-flame-sized *visual* shape, **not actual combustion**: no mandatory fuel, heat, oxygen consumption, burning or smoke. The surface is a continuously evolving **dark-gray recursive shadow** trapping most internal luminosity. Green dominates, so the whole reads as dark green/charcoal with green light intermittently pressing through. Red, Orange, Yellow, Blue and Violet may appear as occasional tiny chromatic sparks/leakage, consistent with the realm's illustrative ~80% Green/~20% other five basin configuration. This is a structural energy profile, not a rigid species color palette or five other Sprite subspecies.

## Collective/hive organization
Sprites can merge into one **hive mind, one collective entity**. Three or four Sprites may form a modest small collective; additional Sprites can simply make a **larger moving recursion-flame** (even bonfire-sized) with more energy and magical capacity, still not burning. No fixed population thresholds or linear power formula are canonized.

The same collective can instead **actually become** any locally admissible functioning biological form (examples: real tree, human, dragon, Leviathan), **not a green glowing shadow imitation, hologram or illusion**. A Sprite-formed tree looks and functions as a genuine tree; a Sprite-formed dragon looks and functions as a genuine dragon. Its underlying identity remains a Green-native Sprite collective rather than an ordinary Red-native dragon; physical morphology does not automatically grant that species' native Transduction specialization.

Collective size and compatible energy must be sufficient to realize and sustain the target form. A Leviathan generally demands many more Sprites than a dragon, and small creatures may require only a few. Energy, Resolution, Bandwidth, admissibility and collective organization constrain feasible expressions. No invented exact equations, fixed Sprite counts, or unrestricted capabilities.

## Magical capability
Sprites inherit Green's existing **realization of admissible perceived potential into locally supported reality**, as formerly exemplified by Kitsune. This is more than illusion. A single Sprite has modest energy/magic; a larger collective can express more. A collective tree can act defensively using Green-native Sprite Transduction. Collective forms do not inherit all powers of the copied species merely by looking and functioning like it.

## Black Sprite
An exceptional **Black Sprite** has a black containment shadow with the full neon rainbow within, not Green-dominant leakage. The author's accepted 'variation' reference in `SPRITE_NATIVE_AND_BLACK_REFERENCE.jpg` represents Black, not an ordinary Green chromatic variation. Its relationship to prophecy is story-level and under development. Its crystal containment is in `03_BLACK_SPRITE_CRYSTAL.md`.
