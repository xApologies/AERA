# AERA — Human physiological expressions and Neurogenesis development

**Status:** author-established AERA optional human-variation model, 2026-10-13. **Layer:** L2 biological inheritance, dependent on L1 Neurogenesis. **Current labels:** dwarf-like, human-like, elf-like; replacement in-world names OPEN. These are physiological expressions/subgroups of one underlying humanity, not three mandatory independent fantasy species.

## Population-level typical development

| Provisional human expression | Typical innate neural Genesis-channel development | Implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dwarf-like | Relatively low development | Direct conscious Genesis interaction tends to be harder/less available without training or augmentation. |
| Human-like | Intermediate/generalized development | Ordinary versatile access, dependent on individual training and knowledge. |
| Elf-like | Relatively high development | More readily supports native Transduction; elf-like practitioners can be natural transductionists, though expertise still requires learning. |

These are **population-level tendencies within this optional AERA human model**, not absolute castes, species-specific worth, guaranteed talent, or proof that any individual can/cannot use magic. All three inherit the same basic six-strand Neurogenesis principle and are subject to individual variation, conceptual knowledge, Resolution, Bandwidth, fatigue, and field admissibility. A highly trained dwarf-like individual may outperform an untrained elf-like individual; no hard numeric ratio, channel diameter or proficiency rank is established.

## Compatibility
- Does not create separate chromatic basins or operational Transduction domains.
- Does not require elf-like individuals to use only native manipulation, nor prohibit dwarf-like individuals from Bindings, Genesis Cards, potions or White Awakening where otherwise admissible.
- Distinguish **innate channel development** from **learned manifold proficiency**, wealth, profession, or social status.
- Existing AERA human anatomy and author-selectable worldbuilding remain intact. Other authors may adopt, rename, alter or omit these human expressions.
- The existing L2 overview's warning against inferring ability from species *labels alone* remains important: this is an explicitly chosen physiological model with statistical tendencies, not an unqualified rule for all settings.
