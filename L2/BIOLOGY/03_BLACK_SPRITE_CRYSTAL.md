# Black Sprite Crystal — accepted artifact design / MK231 proposal

**Visual status:** ACCEPTED by author as baseline on 2026-10-13. **Story status:** proposed replacement for MK231's first Black Phoenix Egg Binding; do not automatically revise MK231.

## Exterior
Elongated, approximately crystal-like, irregular but **planar faceted** black object, refined edges but neither perfectly orthogonal cube, sphere/orb, standard cut diamond nor jagged rough rock. Surface resembles deep abyssal black glass/crystal. The reference art's 10–15 cm scale is illustrative **not canonically locked**.

## Interior
A Black Sprite as a **seemingly static / suspended contained recursion**, like a captured flame that has ceased visibly evolving. Full neon rainbow of six chromatic energies is deep inside black, faintly luminous as though trapped beneath an event horizon. The rainbow is contained and does not project as free exterior flames. Avoid ordinary Green-dominant appearance.

## Story significance and non-automatic migration
The author is considering Kira finding this crystal instead of the Black Phoenix Egg and making her **first Binding** with the contained Black Sprite. The Black Sprite's emergence is conceived as a response from the Green realm to the Black Prophecy. The full mechanism and consequences are not yet decided. Existing MK231's Black Phoenix Egg, prophecy-trigger chronology, healing of Kira's Affliction, first Binding, companion sequence and Coherence/Decoherence pairing remain authoritative **for MK231 until explicitly changed**.

AERA's eight literary prophecy manuscripts have been searched for literal Phoenix Egg references and do not explicitly require one. Their abstract language can accommodate this artifact without editing the prophecies. Do not assert that this crystal definitely caused the Unicorn to speak or that Kira already has Green's powers as an accepted story revision.
