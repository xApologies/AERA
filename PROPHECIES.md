# AERA — The Eight Prophecies of Magic

**Framework-level magical description and shared fictional history.** Seven named ancient prophecies describe broad foundations and constraints of magic; the unique eighth Black Prophecy describes **the singular completion of magic**. They are **NOT** six chromatic basins or six operational Transduction domains, and they do not force particular races or magic schools upon authors.

| # | Canonical named prophecy | Fundamental operation | Principal constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | **The Seven Realms** | Exist across realm structures; structural existence / compatibility | Compatibility |
| II | **The Unwritten** | Express valid statements/meaning through inscriptions, words, Genesis grammar | Grammar and meaning |
| III | **Borrowed Hours** | Transfer duration / temporal interactions | Temporal obligations |
| IV | **Thousand Threads** | Modify relationships between identities | Existing connections |
| V | **Covenant Gardens** | Negotiate agreements and relational rights | Consent and obligation |
| VI | **Living Art** | Cultivate magical life | Biology and ecology |
| VII | **Cartographers** | Alter spatial adjacency | Geometric consistency |
| VIII | **The Black Prophecy** | Singular AERA historical completion of magic through Kira's Black manifestation | Its detailed wording/constraint is not fixed |

**Historical naming:** The old user screenshot states *Six Realms*, and singular *Covenant Garden*. Later workshop specifically corrected Six → **Seven Realms** and previously recorded **Covenant Gardens** as plural. In a recent explanatory recitation the singular form reappeared. Do not silently overwrite plural from the old screenshot. Keep this as a minor naming ambiguity for author confirmation if needed, without delaying AERA publication. `Borrowed Hours` is not a new 'Bard Hours' prophecy.

### Scope of the First Prophecy

The **Seven Realms** concerns existence's six chromatic structural energies and White superposition, not merely travel inside seven geography zones or a closed set of seven inhabited worlds. Magic exists within the structure of existence; the seven concepts together describe magic itself, not magic restricted to MK231's six chosen chromatic populations.

### The Black Prophecy's exceptional place in AERA

The *Black Phoenix Egg* first appears in an Orange-native Phoenix energetic-reproduction event, prompting ancient unicorn prophetic knowledge. Much later within Kira's personal chronology, Kira binds the egg's independent Phoenix identity instead of receiving a conventional therapeutic Shaping. The first Black shared-existence bond activates a network of other Black realizations across otherwise independently timed chromatic realms. Kira, manifestation of Black's entire normalized neon spectrum, establishes six relationships with Black Phoenix, Kitsune, Dragon, Unicorn, Primordial and Leviathan. Her **reciprocal amplification** strengthens their expressions while giving her selected access to their capabilities. Together they **complete magic**. A secondary vague prophetic reference foretells an individual who may **heal existence**, including remediation of Kira's Starscape's abnormally high Yellow-neon problem and its collapse-driven ELDRIS threat. The exact actual prophetic verses and mathematical repair procedure remain **unwritten/OPEN**.

The unique Black Prophecy is a **framework-wide shared-fictional-continuity event**, rather than a power template available again in future sagas. Later AERA stories can treat it as ancient history, legend, misunderstood narrative or distant news. An author may choose their work's relationship to the shared continuity; they cannot claim a second 'original Black singularity' in that continuity without explicitly modifying its canon.

### Interpretations across time

The unicorns of MK231 preserve/interpret the prophecies particularly deeply, but their in-world knowledge is not perfect. A distant future culture may only remember fragments. Other fictional worlds can have different populations, schools and traditions. No human requires pre-existing knowledge of ancient eight-verse poetry in order to use Transduction; the Prophecies describe the overall magical architecture and its history.

**Literary provenance:** These are *authorial fantasy statements* under AERA, not claims about supernatural events demonstrably occurring in Earth's physical history. No debate about real-world prophecy is required for story production.
