# AERA maintenance decision — human physiological expressions

The author establishes an optional AERA human-variation model: dwarf-like, human-like, elf-like are expressions of one humanity, with typically low, intermediate, and high innate Genesis-channel development, respectively. This explains a natural Transduction advantage among elf-like people while retaining individual variation and training requirements. Provisional race labels remain until renamed. No new mathematical equations, hard limits, caste hierarchy, compulsory species or MK231 story edits are authorized.

Companion patch: Genesis cards have collectible-only and operational variants; operational cards can themselves be traded/collected. Historical provenance stays historical. The two updates are documented independently and may be published together in this one package.
