# Afflicted — optional inherited Genesis-biological condition

**AERA scope:** Optional Layer Two biological realization inheriting Layer Zero chromatic/singularity conditions and Layer One Neurogenesis and Awakening. Not an exclusively MK231 or Kaira-specific mutation.

## Cosmological context
In an author-selected Starscape where singularity superposition has unusually high **Neon affinity**, an inherited Afflicted mutation can arise in the population. The setting may associate that condition with ELDRIS and Genesis recursive-collapse instability. This is a proposed **fictional causal context**, not a mathematically derived genetic mechanism, not proof of a universal singularity defect, and not a requirement for every AERA Starscape. Do not conflate Black, White superposition, and the six chromatic basin identities.

## Inherited phenotype and mechanism
Afflicted individuals inherit an unusual Genesis-channel organization: their six Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Violet structures exhibit bounded recursive containment. The channels appear charcoal/dark gray to black, with intermittent faint leakage in the corresponding basin color, analogous in *appearance/mechanism of containment* to native Sprite recursion. Afflicted humans are not Sprites or ELDRIS. Inheritance, penetrance, clinical progression and genetic details remain OPEN.

## White Awakening of an Afflicted
A White Awakening Potion remakes the individual's Genesis biology and physiology, but **does not cause the preexisting Afflicted recursive condition**. The Awakened Afflicted retains their underlying recursive channel identity. The generic Awakened phenotype applies: healthy multilayered skin retaining the individual's natural pigmentation; healthy hair retaining its natural color; original iris color with faint internally contained luminescence; leaner, denser and somewhat more angular/symmetrical anatomy; integrated neural/Genesis biology; and six cutaneous chromatic channels called **aen**.

When an Awakened Afflicted consciously expresses aen, they appear as dark/black branching, fractal or expanded cutaneous anchor structures with occasional subtle basin-specific light leakage. At high proficiency, aen may cover much of the body, enhancing energy intake by enlarging accessible anchoring surface. Aen are functional field anchors, not tattoo pigments or flames. Resting aen need not be visible. Aen mastery is trained, not automatically granted by potion.

The same biological rules apply to all compatible Afflicted people regardless of wealth, social class or skin color. A noble able to afford Awakening can have the same transformation as Kaira. Do not give all Awakened Afflicted unique Black/Neon powers or Kaira-specific destiny.

## MK231 worked example, NOT universal requirement
**Kaira** (canonical K-A-I-R-A; dictated “Kira” refers to Kaira) survived Affliction to age **23**, notably beyond the MK231 typical 11–17-year lifespan. She is unusual in survival and circumstances, not in the underlying transformation mechanism. She retains naturally very pale/white skin, white hair and steel-gray eyes with faint internal white luminosity; activated aen are black recursive channels over her white skin. Wealthy Afflicted people can undergo the same general Awakening earlier. Her older Black spectral-eye account is superseded in this current workshop, but do not edit the MK231 repository without a separate migration decision.
