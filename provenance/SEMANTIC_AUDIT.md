# Semantic audit and source lineage — v0.4.0

## Why this pass exists

AERA's initial `PROPHECIES.md` preserved the seven names and operation/constraint table, but did not preserve the full recovered semantic elaborations. The author requested an exhaustive author-facing semantic pass **independent of the later prophecy narrative**. This release expands explanations without inventing formulas or changing the framework.

## Recovered source details not to lose

- Unwritten: language/semantics, ru’ne, sigils and concepts are examples, not identical systems.
- Borrowed Hours: durations far beyond literal hours; Time Shell connection; speculative five-second/three-second prior-configuration example explicitly exploratory.
- Thousand Threads: relations between identities/realms/time/material geometry; relational connectedness distinct from spatial adjacency; exact create/sever/strengthen permissions OPEN.
- Covenant Gardens: agreements among conscious parties vs metaphorical compatibility of non-conscious structures; **no spirits/consenting mountains**.
- Living Art: magic may be grown/bred/grafted; a magical expression may be alive without being conscious; not all magic is alive.
- Cartographers: spatial adjacency, nonordinary continuous geometry, boundaries, admissible mapping; not automatic universal portals.
- Seven Realms: structure of existence rather than seven separate magic schools.

## Important incompleteness, not license to invent

This archive is exhaustive with respect to the *available recovered author-level meanings*, not a completed mathematical formalization. Where MK231 itself marks a law OPEN, we preserve it. All elaboration marked INTERPRETIVE is explanatory only and requires author acceptance before promotion to ESTABLISHED mechanics.

## Naming

Current MK231 source uses `Covenant Gardens` plural; historical screenshot and occasional user recitation use singular. Preserve existing published canonical plural and note ambiguity without treating it as a substantive conceptual conflict. Seven Realms supersedes Six Realms.
