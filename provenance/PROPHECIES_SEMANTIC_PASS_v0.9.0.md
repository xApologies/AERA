## 1 — Covenant Garden(s): substantive correction
Earlier classification: `Negotiate agreements / Consent and obligation`; historical contractual/negotiated-powers idea. The author expressly rejected contractual magic as the defining pillar and redirected this domain toward **the relationship between magic and living systems, and the question of existence itself**. The fundamental inquiry is: what is life as a system within existence, and what is the role of magic in that relationship? Whether existence requires a witness or experience remains an *open philosophical question*, NOT a cosmological law or a claim that observers create existence.

**New author-level working description:** `Magic's relationship with living existence / Conditions and mystery of living participation`. Avoid inventing a mandatory consent operator, sentient mountains, binding contracts, observer-dependent physics, or a biological consciousness requirement. Consent/obligation remains *historical provenance* and may be relevant in a specific story, but is not the universal core of this prophecy.

Naming: current framework docs say `Covenant Gardens` (plural), accepted literary manuscript says `The Covenant Garden` (singular). Preserve both faithfully. Do not silently rename canon; mark this as a naming variation pending author's future decision.

## 2 — Living Art: substantive correction
Earlier classification: `Cultivate magical life / Biology and ecology` is now too narrow. The author expanded it to **what it means for a living system to be expressed**, encompassing organisms and dynamically evolving, self-organizing systems. A recursive topology under closure may be perceived as living-like without being biologically alive or conscious. The Genesis Field's resolution, bandwidth, organization, and dynamic recursion are author-level analogies, NOT proofs that every recursion is life.

**New author-level working description:** `Expression and evolution of living systems / Structural organization, continuity and transformation`. Biological flora/fauna, Genesis-adapted/dependent life, ecosystems and cultivation remain valid examples, not exhaustive definitions. Preserve the distinction: **Covenant Garden concerns the relationship between magic and living existence; Living Art concerns the expression, organization and evolution of living systems.** Do not assert that all mathematical systems are conscious or biological.

## 3 — Seven concepts and the eighth: literary/metaphysical distinction
The first seven are foundational pillars describing different aspects of magic. The Eighth Black Prophecy is an independent **foretelling of the singular manifestation/existence of magic**, not a mandatory eighth operational domain or a formula that literally 'unifies seven powers'. The eventual fictional realization is associated with Kira in MK231, but the prophecy text must NOT name Kira, species, Phoenix Egg or disclose the event mechanically. Preserve existing MK231 story examples as *story-level* material, separate from the prophecy's wording.

The prophetic literary voice: **Magic is the author; the Unicorn is the voice; the prophecy is the revelation.** The Unicorn is not a first-person narrator describing experiences or teaching. Text is continuous, impersonal, mythic, ambiguous, winding and recursively referential, not a sequence of parables, numbered scenes or didactic short stories. This is a **literary convention for AERA's prophecy corpus**, not a global rule for all prose.
