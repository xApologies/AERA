**Author workshop:** November 8, 2026. **Primary owner:** `L2/ALCHEMY_VAEN.md`. **Cross-reference as appropriate:** `L2/GENESIS_BIOLOGY.md`, `L2/GENESIS_ENVIRONMENTS.md`, `L2/INHERITANCE/02_ALCHEMY_VAEN_AND_BINDING.md`, `CONCEPTS/08_CROSSWALK.md`. ## Current established definition to preserve
`vaen` are naturally occurring specialty resources with unusual Genesis-relevant identities/properties, separate from ordinary chemical elements, high-energy material, Genesis crystals/dust and the Type 1–5 material-science taxonomy. They can bear multiple identities and may be used by compatible optional Binding Circle implementations. Existing examples *Voidbloom Lotus* and *Teardrop of the Everlasting Star* remain valid; don't change them or their story provenance.

## Author-selected refinement: two-stage Genesis evolution
**Native White object/material → first Genesis expression → second identity-bearing vaen realization.** The first event is a field-mediated transformation, biological evolution/adaptation in biological cases, or analogous geological/material reorganization in nonliving cases. The second event is a distinct Genesis interaction with environmental geometry/pressure, establishing **specialized identity-bearing geometry** within the entire item or a particular component. Genesis expression alone is insufficient. A glowing fungus is not necessarily vaen. The identity need not be globally unique, extremely rare, exclusively Green, or a large energy reservoir.

Examples: special Red Arc Awakening mushroom; flowers; a patch of Genesis-expressive fungi with one/two mushrooms becoming vaen; specialized environmentally formed seeds; trees (whole tree or bark, leaves, resin, sap/amber); Genesis-expressive deer with identity-bearing antlers; optional shells; mineral nodules, deposits and stones (Orange Arc moss ecology); Genesis-interacting lightning-formed glass; conditional fossils/mineralized wood. Lichen remains a preferred example of Stage-1 expression rather than selected vaen. Previously excluded antlers and fossils are now conditional possibilities. The frog in Orange Arc is Genesis-expressive fauna, **not vaen**. Not every substrate must be alive, and not every expressed organism has a vaen component.

## Chromatic realm composition — proposed explanation, not L0 amendment
White supplies native material existence. **First evolution:** Violet Genesis Field, Green Resolution, Blue Bandwidth, Yellow Persistence are useful dominant explanatory emphases; Red Chirality and Orange Topology are still part of White. **Second evolution:** Violet further field interaction, Green realization of admissible identity, Red identity organization/chirality, Orange topology/geometry, Yellow persistence, Blue capacity/bandwidth. This is **not a mandatory six-step process** or a new L0 operator. Exact equations, the necessity of Green for every vaen, and the formal placement of Orange Topology are OPEN. Existing MK163 primary realm emphases (Red Chirality, Orange Topology, Yellow Persistence, Green Resolution, Blue Bandwidth, Violet Genesis Field) are source-specific assignments, not universal prophecy schools.
