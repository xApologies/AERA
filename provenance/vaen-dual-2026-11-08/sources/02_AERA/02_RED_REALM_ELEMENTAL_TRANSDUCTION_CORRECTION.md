**Explicit author correction:** November 8, 2026. **Scope:** AERA Red Realm/native Red expression; NOT a global deletion of Coherence/Decoherence as a valid operational domain. **Status:** approved correction, with required reconciliation against other existing Red descriptions.

## Required action
Audit AERA for **Red Realm** / Red-native / Dragon descriptions that incorrectly use **Coherence/Decoherence** as Red's realm-specific specialization or primary magical expression. In those **Red-specific descriptions**, change the specialization to **Elemental Transduction**. Preserve any historical source quotations as historical and document their supersession. Those are NOT the Red Realm assignment being corrected.

## Important source collision to reconcile rather than conceal
MK163's November 7 accepted native specialization map currently says **Red Dragon → Chirality** (`WORLD_CLOCK/L1_FANTASY/OPERATIONAL_DOMAINS.md`), and its six Dragon-associated capabilities include **Elemental Transduction** as one capability. AERA `L2/BIOLOGY/00_READ_FIRST.md` describes six distinct operational specializations among Red Dragons, rather than a single mandatory capability for every dragon. These statements do not by themselves establish that Red Chirality must be removed. **Do not silently overwrite Red Chirality or declare all dragons individually elemental specialists.** Instead:
1. Distinguish **Red realm elemental expression** from **Red Chirality structural emphasis** and from **individual Dragon specializations** if consistent with source.
2. If the author's request is intended to replace the existing MK163 Red→Chirality assignment too, that further scope is not explicitly authorized by this package; flag it as a **pending author decision** and preserve the current MK163 mapping.
3. Do not rewrite the AERA six generic operational domains, seven prophecies, their denizen crosswalk, Orange Vaelith coherence, or other realms.
4. Do not equate elemental fire with unlimited thermal energy; finite Transduction limits and source constraints still apply.
