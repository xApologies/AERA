GENESIS DIAMOND CANON
====================

Identity:
  Battery

Purpose:
  Store Genesis Energy

Role:
  Universal Energy Storage Infrastructure


CORE PRINCIPLE
--------------

The Genesis Diamond is a container.

The energy is not part of the diamond.

The diamond remains diamond.

The energy remains Genesis energy.

The carbon lattice provides a stable
containment geometry.

The energy occupies the available
configuration space within the crystal.

The crystal stores energy.

The crystal does not become energy.

The energy does not become carbon.


ENERGY STORAGE
--------------

Any basin energy may be stored.

  Red
  Orange
  Yellow
  Green
  Blue
   Violet

Energy is introduced into the crystal.

The crystal organizes the stored
occupancy into a stable arrangement.

The exact arrangement is not fixed.

The crystal performs a quicksort-like
organization process.

The crystal does NOT preserve a single
uniform basin value throughout the volume.

The crystal preserves:

===== PAGE 2 =====
   Total Stored Energy

and

  Average Energy Density

through a stable occupancy distribution.

The resulting stored state is safer,
more manageable, and more stable than
the raw basin energy used to charge it.


CHARGE STATES
-------------

0 State
-------

Clear Diamond

No stored Genesis energy.

No color.

No frost.

Simply a diamond.

--------

Average density remains below Red.

(< 1/7)

Appearance:

  Clouded
   Diffuse

---

(~ 1/7)

Appearance:

  Clouded red crystal

WHY DIAMONDS?
-------------

Not because diamond becomes energy.

Not because energy becomes diamond.

The carbon lattice provides an
exceptionally stable containment geometry.

The crystal allows highly energetic
Genesis density to be stored and managed.

Without containment:

  Energy escapes.

With containment:

  Energy becomes usable.


SUPERPOSITION RULE
------------------

The universe naturally exists as:

  Red
  Orange
  Yellow
  Green
  Blue
   Violet

in White Superposition.

Single isolated basin densities are
less stable than the natural superposed state.

The crystal continuously organizes
stored energy into a stable average density.

This allows high-density basin energy
to be safely stored and later utilized.

IMPORTANT NOTE
--------------

These are author-level reference values.

The purpose is economic and infrastructure
consistency.

The exact scene-level charging process is
rarely shown.

The important observation is:

  Higher basin access
  ↓

  Faster charging

  Lower basin access
  ↓

  Slower charging


EXAMPLE
-------

A Red-ranked worker charging
for one hour after work:

  8 days
  ↓

  One Red chip

A Violet-ranked individual:

  1 hour
  ↓

  One Red chip

This creates a meaningful economic
difference between basin access levels.

CANON SUMMARY
-------------

The Genesis Diamond is a battery.

Energy enters the crystal.

The crystal organizes the stored energy
into a stable average density.

Color indicates charge state.

Clear Diamond:
  Empty

Infrared:
  Below Red

Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet


represent increasing average energy density.

Ultraviolet represents density beyond Violet.

The diamond is a container.

The energy is the stored object.

Charging speed depends on basin access.

Crystal size determines storage capacity.

Civilization stores Genesis energy
using Genesis Diamonds.
