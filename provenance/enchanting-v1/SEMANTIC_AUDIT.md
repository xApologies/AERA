## Reverse semantic comparison

| Decision group | Integrated meaning and retained limit |
| --- | --- |
| Object identity | Enchanting alters an object's admissible local Genesis geometry and persistent identity/inheritance. The object remains identifiable unless a specified transformation changes that identity. Enchanting is an author-level name; its in-world name remains OPEN. |
| Persistence | Successful identity modification persists when the preparation ends, an effect deactivates, a conduit disengages or a reserve depletes. Continued powered expression is distinct from identity persistence. This does not settle removal/dispelling/failure or imply destruction immunity. |
| Prepared manufacture | Physical `ru’ne`/sigil substrate, object anchors and designed crystal/dust/vaen inputs support repeatable fabrication. Designated resources are consumed per activation of one coherent enchantment system. A multicomponent flaming edge may be one system; independent enchantments normally require separate operations/resources. No automatic microscopic-property count, slot count or exact recipe is imposed. |
| Direct Architect manufacture | Expert conscious manifold construction and identity resolution may achieve the same persistent result without inherently requiring the prepared route's specialized consumables. Energy, effort, knowledge, admissibility and time still matter; failure is possible. Bespoke work is possible without declaring Architects incapable of standardized production. |
| Lifecycle and preparation | Where conscious Transduction is involved, both routes retain Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release. Fabrication/preparation and later operation remain distinct; no extra activation stage or seventh operation is introduced. |
| Intrinsic versus activated | Intrinsic altered properties need no ongoing activation by default. An activated item permanently carries the capability even when its effect is off. Touch, hilt sigils and conscious input are optional activation arrangements, not mandatory interfaces for all users. |
| Corrected reservoir choice | Finite reserves may be either R–V Genesis crystals or charged Genesis Diamonds. They may be embedded, attached or compatibly coupled; no universal socket or diamond-only battery claim is introduced. Depletion limits expression without deleting identity modification. |
| Conduit distinction | Crystals can act as basin anchors/conduits rather than finite stores. R/O/G for a flaming edge is illustrative, not a universal recipe or one-to-one mapping between energy basins and operational domains. Delivery, stability and throughput still need valid engineering. |
| Composability | Intrinsic, activated, reservoir and conduit configurations are combinable property/operation options, not four magical schools, basins or fabrication routes. Four features do not automatically imply exactly four activations or exactly one. |
| Material stress | Stress, cooldown, maintenance and throughput reduction are optional authored material behavior. They are not automatically biological fatigue or a universal timer, and do not automatically erase the persistent identity. |
| Diamonds and crystals | Diamond lattice/container and stored energy remain distinct. Diamond charge-state color describes average density, not necessarily source-basin identity. Empty/infrared/R–V/ultraviolet do not add energy basins. Other Genesis crystals may serve reserves or anchors without inheriting all Diamond charging rules. |
| Identity resources | Dust and identity-bearing vaen remain distinct material roles. Multiple identities may exist in one vaen and a Binding may select a subset. Earlier source authority that vaen is not the only possible identity input remains unchanged. No new chemistry or potion recipe follows. |
| Prior technology | Ring conservation through inheritance reassignment remains distinct from object-property engineering. Direct persistent object modification is not identical to temporary Architect velis; the existing velis maintenance/load limits remain scoped to those structures. Card manufacture, personal Binding occupancy and source-specific professions are not rewritten. |
| Prior biology and narrative | Neurogenesis, one-per-basin potion default, White Awakening, lowercase `aen`, optional Affliction and all their OPEN boundaries remain intact. Neither Enchanting nor an energy reservoir supplies a new cure, Black power, prophecy revision or story migration. |

## Unresolved interactions and source notes

- **Card-anchor interaction:** global exclusivity across a practitioner's interfaces remains authoritative. How an item's conduits relate to that personal access, and whether a conduit can sustain an effect without a separate reservoir, remain OPEN. The checkpoint supplies neither an exemption from Card exclusivity nor a rule assigning every independent item anchor to its owner. No forced cancellation or unlimited energy supply is invented.
- **Mass/conservation:** the mass-reduced cube/sword is an illustrative fictional property. Weight, inertial mass, gravity, physical matter and energy conservation are not silently equated. Exact laws remain OPEN; L0 is not amended.
- **Identity engineering:** stacking, compatibility, interference, item boundaries, dispelling, removal, failure, activation cost, output/range/losses, repair/material fatigue, sigil geometry, resource quantities, production rates/prices and the in-world name remain OPEN or author-configurable as supplied.
- **Source filename:** the package mentions a supplied seven-page `MASTER.pdf` but contains no PDF. The existing [Diamond source register](../technology-v0.6.0/SOURCE_REGISTER.md) records `MASTER(2).pdf` as the original filename of `GENESIS_DIAMOND_MASTER.pdf`. This checkpoint uses the existing canonical derivative and preserved master; it does not assert the byte identity of an absent differently named file. Its qualitative Diamond claims agree with that derivative, so no source replacement is needed.
- **Inherited matters:** earlier Diamond density/Ring wording discrepancies, biological/Type One/Type Two questions, Kaira workshop versus historical portrayals and separate story migrations retain their prior status. Enchanting does not resolve them.

No blocking contradiction requiring a change to existing mechanics was found. The item-versus-personal-Card boundary is explicitly unresolved, not silently harmonized. Source records retain their own authority; no external repository was cloned, imported or modified.
