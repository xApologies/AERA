============================================================
0. NAME / TERM
============================================================

ELDRIS are dungeon-born entities produced by stabilized recursive Genesis collapse.

They are not demons.
They are not ordinary animals.
They are not elemental creatures.
They are not “red monsters” visually colored red.

They are persistent recursive collapse structures.

If “LRS” is used as shorthand, it refers to the same concept in this context:
a localized recursive structure produced by Genesis/chirality collapse.

Core phrase:

  ELDRIS = stabilized recursive collapse with basin-dominant closure.

============================================================
1. WHAT AN ELDRIS IS
============================================================

An ELDRIS is what happens when a local Genesis instability begins collapsing recursively, but instead of dispersing cleanly back
into the field, the collapse stabilizes into closure.

The collapse does not stop.

It stabilizes.

That is the key.

The ELDRIS is not a finished object in the normal sense.
It is a bounded process that became persistent enough to behave like an entity.

In MK43 / MK147 language:

  ELDRIS = Persistent Recursive Topology
       generated by runaway Genesis collapse
         stabilized through chirality closure.

The environment does not see this as “evil.”

The environment only sees:

  Persistent?
  Stable?
  Closed?

If yes, the structure is admitted.

Civilization sees:

  Monster.

3. WHY THEY LOOK BLACK
============================================================

ELDRIS do not visually appear red, orange, yellow, green, blue, or violet.

They appear black, wispy, rootlike, smoky, branching, fractured, or shadow-structured.

Reason:

  The visible blackness is the recursive collapse signature.

The basin color is trapped inside the collapse.

Normally red energy would look red.
Normally blue energy would look blue.

But inside an ELDRIS, the recursive collapse contains the basin expression so tightly that the color does not freely radiate outward.

So the body appears black.

The color only leaks through in limited markers:

=== PDF PAGE 3 OF 12 ===
  eyes
  claws
  wounds
   inner glow
  breath
  impact effects
  traces along branches/wisps
  behavioral expression

Thus:

  black body = recursive collapse
  colored marker = basin identity leak

Example:

  Red ELDRIS:
     black body
     red eye-trace
     physical persistence behavior

  Blue ELDRIS:
     black body
     blue eye-trace
     explosive / expressive projection behavior

============================================================
5. BASIN CLASSIFICATIONS
============================================================

The basin color tells what kind of closure won.

------------------------------------------------------------
RED ELDRIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Power category: A
Identity: persistence / physicality
Behavior: claws, teeth, melee, body-force
Threat: physical contact

Red ELDRIS are the most basic stable monster expression.

They are not harmless.
They are simply the lowest basin-dominant collapse.

They can still kill people.

They are black-bodied, but their red identity may appear in the eyes or small internal traces.

They are the “fuzzy ball” of monster structure:
low coherence, low density, but still persistent.

------------------------------------------------------------
ORANGE ELDRIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Power category: A
Identity: energy intrusion
Behavior: physical contact plus energy injection
Threat: getting touched becomes much worse

Orange ELDRIS are still contact-based.

The tactical language is similar to Red:

  Do not get touched.

But Orange adds intrusion.

A claw strike is not just a claw strike.
The creature can push basin energy into the target.

This causes fatigue, destabilization, internal damage, or healing-load pressure.

------------------------------------------------------------
YELLOW ELDRIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Power category: A
Identity: energy-mass projection
Behavior: orbs, beams, rings, projected force
Threat: ranged force

Yellow introduces ranged danger.

Its attacks are not pure light.
They carry force.

=== PDF PAGE 5 OF 12 ===
An orb can knock someone down.
A beam can dent armor.
A ring can hit like an expanding wall.

This is still Category A on the broad power scale, but it changes the logistics of fighting.

The group now needs:

  cover
  spacing
  shields
   barriers
   line-of-sight discipline

------------------------------------------------------------
GREEN ELDRIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Power category: B
Identity: resonance / structural reinforcement
Behavior: juggernaut persistence
Threat: professional threshold

Green is the first major power jump.

Green ELDRIS are not simply Yellow plus extra abilities.

They are resonance-upgraded persistence structures.

They are often much larger:
nine-foot juggernauts, heavy bodies, reinforced topology.

They are difficult to kill because the structure resists collapse.

A group might take an hour to bring down one Green ELDRIS.

Green is where dungeon delving becomes a professional operation.

------------------------------------------------------------
BLUE ELDRIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Power category: B
Identity: expressed energy projection
Behavior: explosions, burning beams, secondary effects
Threat: energy does something after impact

Blue is the advanced expression of the Yellow family.

Yellow force hits.

Blue expression acts.

A Yellow orb knocks you back.
A Blue orb explodes.

A Yellow beam pushes into armor.
A Blue beam burns through armor.

A Yellow ring hits like a truck.
A Blue ring may detonate, shear, burn, or restructure the space it crosses.

Blue is where formation discipline becomes brutally important.

=== PDF PAGE 6 OF 12 ===
------------------------------------------------------------
VIOLET ELDRIS
------------------------------------------------------------

Power category: C
Identity: high Genesis expression
Behavior: mage-like manifestation
Threat: legendary / poorly understood

Violet is not merely stronger Blue.

Violet is where the ELDRIS begins behaving like a true Genesis-expression entity.

Most people do not understand Violet ELDRIS.

They know they exist.
They know they are terrifying.
They know not to fight them casually.

But detailed knowledge is rare.

Violet is legend-tier.
