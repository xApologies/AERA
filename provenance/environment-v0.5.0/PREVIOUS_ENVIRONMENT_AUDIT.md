## Finding

The principal environmental systems are present in AERA: chromatic existence and neon configuration, Genesis collapse, eldris, persistent dungeons, Genesis storms, domai, Genesis-active biology, resources, and settlement-protection technologies. Their definitions are not merely missing topic names.

The published environmental chapter is nevertheless a compressed synthesis, not a complete recovery of all source detail. One containment rule has been weakened in wording. Several source-defined details about domai, eldris and dungeon behavior are not explicit in the reviewed AERA chapters. Those are identified below without inventing replacement mechanics.

**Verdict: core environmental inheritance preserved; targeted semantic-detail restoration warranted.** This is not a claim of zero information loss across every repository or historical file.

## Targeted corrections and additions

### ENV-01 — Restore the strength of dungeon containment

**Source:** S2 and S4 describe eldris as effectively confined to compatible strata.  
**Destination:** A1 currently says they 'tend to stay' in their strata.

That is not equivalent. 'Tend to stay' reads as a behavioral preference and permits casual roaming outside; effective confinement is the inherited environmental basis for the comparatively safe surrounding settlements. Restore the source meaning for the inherited dungeon implementation. Do not upgrade it into an invented theorem about every possible author-designed dungeon or add exceptions not supplied by the source.

**Suggested destination:** A1, 'Dungeons — related but distinct'; cross-reference A4's delver communities.

### ENV-02 — Preserve dungeon oscillations and noncombat outputs

The master, section 12, describes structured Genesis environments with oscillating admissible states. Possible outputs include monsters, flowers, crystals, cores, rare configurations, treasure-bloom and saturation events. A no-monster crystal outcome is an explicit example. The following page says configurations follow admissibility, not fairness.

AERA already preserves persistent dungeon stratification and the option of dungeons without eldris. It does not yet preserve this richer local operating description in A1. Add it as inherited environmental behavior while retaining the author's ability to set local geography and conditions. Do not turn the master's illustrative population counts into fixed spawn tables, respawn timers or guaranteed harvests.

### ENV-03 — Restore domai's complete source lifecycle and perception

Retain the following distinctions from S1/S2:

- A localized storm is not automatically a domai.
- Natural expiration and core destruction are distinct causes of unraveling.
- The White core is structural, not an eldris commander.
- Unraveling is progressive and supports a final high-risk harvest/evacuation interval.
- Source lifespan generally increases with basin, but exact timers remain unspecified.
- Nearby depth remains usable; distant depth degrades toward a blurred, surface-like projection. Do not import a fixed visibility distance from MK157's separate training story.

Some of these statements are already preserved and must not be rewritten unnecessarily. Restore only the omitted source details. The qualitative lifespan trend belongs to the inherited model; it is not permission to generate a numeric table.

### ENV-04 — Expand eldris substance and behavior without importing a game

A1 has the formation definition and all six family names. Preserve S6/master's additional details: energy-mass closure rather than ordinary flesh; physical collision and dispersal; Orange intrusion causing fatigue/destabilization/internal damage; Yellow force-bearing projections distinct from Blue's expressed after-effects; Green structural reinforcement and sustained-combat pressure; identification by behavior and traces. State the inherited no-White-eldris palette explicitly rather than making future readers infer it from a six-row table.

Do not import MK157's 209 encounter groups, numerical rewards, Trials, respawn rules, character schedules, or the older Kira/illi plot. Those are not required to recover the environmental ontology.

### ENV-05 — Restore the Wilds definition and keep civic examples optional

S3 states that the Wilds describes Genesis-risk outside reliably stabilized territory, not a biome. This is useful reusable environmental language and is currently not explicit in the reviewed environmental chapters.

The source's four-corners-plus-center array, approximate 200-mile coverage, city dimensions, growth timetable, capitals and Village Hub hierarchy are examples of a particular civilization. They can be archived as a sourced optional example without turning them into universal AERA limits. The same applies to the Red/Orange local response and Yellow-plus military pattern in S1.

### ENV-06 — Terminology cleanup

A4 contains `dungeon/dmai excursions`. Correct only that misspelling to `dungeon/domai excursions`. The canonical term is `domai`; a realm, a political domain, a dungeon and a domai are not interchangeable names.

## Existing source conflicts and intentional exclusions

The supplied master calls eldris 'dungeon-born' in some historical passages. S5 explicitly excludes dungeon-born wording, and S2/S4 allow eldris wherever suitable collapse stabilizes. A1 correctly follows that later scoped correction. Do **not** restore the historical restriction during a fuller master import.

The master contains a spirit analogy. S6 limits its import and states it does not introduce a new spirit system. That is not authorization to add spirits, personhood or new afterlife mechanics during this environmental audit.

MK147's King's Tournament, named settlements, nobility, and exact political geography are optional setting material. Their omission from the universal environmental chapter is not a defect. A dedicated examples library can retain them later when requested.

Exact environmental frequencies, area/visibility limits, local calendars, numerical neon thresholds and terrain populations remain author-defined or source-qualified. They are not audit failures needing fabricated values.

ER Foam remains a placeholder. No ER Foam mechanism has been reconstructed in this audit.
