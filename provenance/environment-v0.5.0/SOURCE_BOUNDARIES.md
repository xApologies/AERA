## Why this note is included

The user supplied the eldris master as definitive authority and authorized the prior environmental audit's corrections. The PDF is preserved intact. Its detailed entity definitions guide this pass. The source also includes setting-specific or historically qualified statements that must remain visible rather than be silently deleted, generalized or treated as a fresh unqualified reversal of AERA's current framework.

This conflict is documented, not hidden.

If the author later explicitly reinstates a dungeon-only AERA rule, record that as a separate narrow revision rather than quietly changing the source history.

## Spirits / identity persistence

The master §11 explicitly compares eldris with recognizable identity-bearing persistence after biological death. That material is retained in [Source Context Notes](SOURCE_CONTEXT_NOTES.md) and the original. No part of the source has been censored or dropped from the archive.

The current request is an eldris/environment audit repair, not an instruction to introduce a mandatory populated afterlife or a new universal spirit subsystem. The comparison therefore remains a **preserved source model**. It does not silently alter the seven concepts, Covenant Gardens' agency boundaries, or AERA's current characters. No new “spirit field,” consent law, immortality guarantee or soul taxonomy is invented.

## No-White restriction

The master enumerates Red–Violet families and A/B/C categories. The explicit “no White eldris” sentence comes from the previously audited MK157 general reference. The prior audit requested this inherited palette clarification. The active document cites that supplemental source separately. White core material and a White eldris category are not equivalent.

## Framework scope
