# AERA World Clock — The five-layer framework data structure

**Author-corrected definition:** The **World Clock IS the stack of layers L0 through L4**. It does not have to be a separate central timeline, scheduling application or planetary calendar. Do not demand a sixteenth 'world-state reconciliation' or a mandatory numerical temporal conversion just because the author hasn't picked one.

1. **L0 Mathematics/physics:** Genesis Field, energy basins, Resolution, Bandwidth, persistence, Chirality Fabric, mathematical topology, Time Shell, Rainbow Road.
2. **L1 Fantasy Engine:** magic through universal Transduction, six operational domains and their author-selected access/shape mechanisms; Genesis biology and material interactions as fictional permissions.
3. **L2 World Engine = Environment Engine + Civilization Engine:** starsystems, terrain, ecology, conditions, languages, agriculture, cities, technology, politics, economy, culture and Institutions of Knowledge.
4. **L3 Character Engine:** organisms/persons/actors, their local biology, cognition, motivation, relationships, choices and capabilities, not prescribed species.
5. **L4 Story Engine:** how characters and environments evolve as a narrative, causality, conflicts, arcs, books, prose.

These layers describe *how a fictional setting exists and functions for its author.* AERA does not pre-fill environments, characters or narratives. Systems in upper layers inherit constraints from lower layers, but a thematic metaphor does not rewrite L0 math.

## Calendars / time

An author MAY use planetary orbits, 31-hour days, an 11-month calendar, multiple world histories, or independent realm-local times, as MK147/MK231 choose. They are **story-specific choices**. The temporal correspondence between worlds is an L0/L1 transport + story implementation question, not an additional required universal World Clock data type. An author can construct a second chronological ledger as a project tool, but that is not what the AERA 'World Clock' universally means.
