# Character Engine — modular biological/identity/agency space

## Author-defined variables, NOT required character templates

- **Biology/physiology**: locally evolved under environmental/gravity/biological constraints; may include ordinary and Genesis-adapted/dependent processes.
- **Cognition/experience**: conceptual models, learning, training, neuroplastic development, memory, fatigue and sensory perception; don't presume the same cognition for all members of a species.
- **Genesis access**: none, one basin, several, six; inherited, awakened, Bound, biological channels or engineered interface as chosen in the setting.
- **Interface**: e.g. Binding Circles, fractals/tattoos, stones, cards, direct geometry, living relationship; must remain compatible with the universal Transduction lifecycle.
- **Agency/relationships**: decisions, obligations, consent, cultures, political roles, friendships, disagreements and personal goals.
- **Growth**: conceptual expertise, stamina, experience, cooperative abilities, optional technological tools and the character's own chosen life direction.

### Human physiology example

AERA's workshop used dwarf-like, ordinary-human-like and elf-like **human expressions**, all parts of humanity's underlying biology, with differences in stature, musculature, neural development/brain adaptation and environmental evolutionary histories. That *does not* mandate a dwarf profession of smith, an elf spellcasting talent, or a different intrinsic soul/type of magic. It does not require every author to have dwarves or elves.

### Native chromatic populations are optional author choices

MK231 chose Red Dragons, Orange Phoenixes, Yellow Primordials, Green Kitsune, Blue Leviathans, Violet Unicorns and White humanity. Authors elsewhere may populate those six domains differently or use basins without navigable realms. In MK231, chromatic species may learn humanoid cultural competency for diplomacy with White without changing their independent identities.

### Kira is a particular worked example

Kira, in the unique Black Prophecy story, is manifestation of the complete Black neon spectrum, a reciprocal universal amplifier through six autonomous Black companions. The Binding is **not** an ownership contract: conscious individuals require consent, and unhatched/cradled beings require guardianship/trust decisions. Each companion retains personhood and native racial identity. This is an exceptional L3 mechanism under the **framework-level unique historical event**, never a mandatory template for all stories.

A character might be a master mathematician, a skilled alchemist, a card-wielder, a mundane farmer, an architect or a nonhuman animal. None requires a novel 'character engine math' here; an author defines characters within AERA's existing lower-layer constraints.
