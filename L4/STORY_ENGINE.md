# The AERA Story Engine

L4 represents the author-controlled narrative emerging from the chosen L0 Mathematics → L1 Fantasy → L2 World → L3 Characters. AERA determines neither protagonist nor genre style beyond the offered high-fantasy framework. Another author may write one contained novella, seven expansive books, hundreds of interplanetary journeys or a scientific academy story.

### Meaning of the World Clock for L4

The World Clock **is the complete five-layer stack**. Authors may keep event ledgers, chapter timestamps, relative histories, maps, relationships and world-state tracks to remain consistent across a complex saga, but AERA does not impose one master calendar or a universal numerical time law.

### MK231 example (NOT universal requirement)

MK231 uses seven narratively colored books Red → Orange → Yellow → Green → Blue → Violet → White. Their companion-native realm colors differ. Kira's Black Prophecy is a one-time AERA shared-continuity event, developing through six independent relationships and ending in 'the completion of magic' / potential healing of existence. Independent realm-local clocks help prevent companions from conveniently appearing the same age all at once. These are **MK231 chosen story features**.
